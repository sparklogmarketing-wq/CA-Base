## Why Professional GST Compliance Support Pays for Itself

Most GST exposure today is created by data, not intent. Vendor invoices missing from GSTR-2B silently erode input tax credit; classification errors surface as notices years later, with interest; missed rate changes distort contract pricing. Our GST compliance services run on reconciliation-first workflows — every credit verified before it is claimed, every product mapped to the correct HSN and rate, with an exposure register showing where risk sits. That discipline turns compliance into working-capital protection.

## The Compliance Cycle We Manage

| Return / form | Who files it | Frequency | Standard due date |
|---|---|---|---|
| GSTR-1 (outward supplies) | Regular taxpayers | Monthly (or quarterly under QRMP) | 11th of the following month; 13th after quarter under QRMP |
| IFF (optional invoice furnishing) | QRMP taxpayers | First two months of each quarter | 13th of the following month |
| GSTR-3B (summary return) | Regular taxpayers | Monthly (or quarterly under QRMP) | 20th of the following month; 22nd or 24th after quarter under QRMP |
| PMT-06 (tax payment) | QRMP taxpayers | First two months of each quarter | 25th of the following month |
| CMP-08 | Composition scheme taxpayers | Quarterly | 18th after the quarter |
| GSTR-9 / GSTR-9C | Annual return; reconciliation where applicable | Annual | 31 December following the financial year |

Behind the calendar sits the real work: reconciling purchases against GSTR-2B, chasing defaulting vendors, verifying e-invoicing and e-way bill data, and reviewing refunds. Every figure in your GSTR-3B should trace back to books that would survive a scrutiny notice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What GST services do businesses outsource most often?

Registration, monthly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filing, ITC reconciliation against GSTR-2B, refund claims and notice handling are the most outsourced GST services, followed by annual GSTR-9 support and audit assistance.

### How did the September 2025 GST rate change affect compliance?

The two-slab structure of 5 percent and 18 percent, plus a 40 percent demerit rate, required remapping HS codes, revising price lists and reclassifying supplies — and it created fresh classification queries we resolve through advisory.

### Do small businesses need to file GST returns every month?

No. Taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to Rs 5 crore can opt for the QRMP scheme and file quarterly returns, with a monthly tax payment through PMT-06 in the first two months of each quarter.

### Can your team represent us before GST officers?

Yes. As chartered accountants we draft replies to ASMT-10 and DRC-01 notices, attend personal hearings under section 75 and file appeals under section 107 on behalf of clients.
