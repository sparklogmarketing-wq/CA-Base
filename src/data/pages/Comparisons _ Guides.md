## LLP vs Private Limited Company: The Comparison Founders Ask For

The most requested comparison is LLP versus private limited company, and the answer depends on intent. **Fundraising:** investors subscribe to equity shares with ESOP pools, so a private limited company is the default for venture-backed ventures. **Compliance load:** every private company carries a mandatory statutory audit and full ROC calendar, while an LLP needs an audit only after crossing prescribed turnover or contribution thresholds. **Taxation:** an LLP is taxed at 30 per cent plus surcharge and cess with no dividend layer, whereas a company can elect the concessional 22 per cent regime under section 115BAA but its distributions face dividend taxation in shareholders' hands. **Foreign investment:** both structures can receive FDI under the automatic route in most sectors, subject to FEMA reporting. Conversions between the two are possible, but doing it early is cheaper than restructuring later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is better for raising venture capital, an LLP or a private limited company?
A private limited company. Venture investors take equity shares and expect ESOP structures, and funds rarely back LLPs. Incorporate as a company if external equity is on the roadmap.

### Is an audit mandatory for every LLP?
No. An LLP requires an audit only when turnover or contribution crosses the prescribed thresholds, though lenders and counterparties may contractually demand audited statements earlier.

### Which ITR form should a freelancer file?
Freelancers claiming presumptive taxation under section 44ADA file ITR-4 where eligible; those with capital gains, foreign income or regular books of account file ITR-3.

### Where can I check the current GST rate of my product?
