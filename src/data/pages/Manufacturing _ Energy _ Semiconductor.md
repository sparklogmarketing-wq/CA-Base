> Tax and compliance for manufacturing companies in India centre on GST input tax credit on capital goods, deemed-export and PLI incentive frameworks, depreciation on new machinery, energy-transition reporting and sector-specific audits. Semiconductor fabs and electronics units add policy-linked incentives that reward disciplined documentation from the first capital purchase.

## Why Is Tax and Compliance for Manufacturing Companies in India Different?

Manufacturing is capital-intensive before it is revenue-generative. Machinery, land and working capital are committed years ahead, while incentive schemes, credit rules and depreciation positions decide whether project economics survive contact with reality. Add energy-intensive processes, contract labour and export linkages, and the compliance surface is wider than most service businesses ever see. The sections below cover the decisions that most affect cash flow and audit outcomes.

## How Does GST Work on Capital Goods and Inputs?

Input tax credit is the largest working-capital lever in a plant's GST account. Credit on plant and machinery is generally available under Section 16 — provided registration exists before purchases, supplier invoices reflect in GSTR-2B, and no blocked credit applies under Section 17(5). Capital-goods credit unwinds: on sale, demolition or liquidation, reversal runs under Section 18. Reverse-charge procurements, imports and capital transfers each carry their own documentation trails.

| GST touchpoint | What discipline requires |
|---|---|
| Capital purchases | Register before buying; capture ITC in the period the invoice appears in GSTR-2B |
| Vendor onboarding | Verify supplier GSTIN, e-invoicing status and credit-worthiness before large contracts |
| Movements | E-way bill from Rs 50,000 consignment value; e-invoicing applies from Rs 5 crore aggregate turnover |
| Exports and supplies to SEZ units | Zero-rated routes with refund of unutilised credit or LUT billing |

Where credit positions are complex — mixed supplies, reversals, RCM intensity — our [GST ITC and RCM guide](/gst-itc-rcm) sets out the mechanics, and our [e-invoicing and e-way bill practice](/gst-e-invoicing-eway-bill) handles the transaction-level plumbing.

## What Are Deemed Exports and Who Benefits?

Deemed exports under Section 2(84) of the IGST Act are goods manufactured in India and supplied against projects or procurements notified as such — classically those funded by multilateral or bilateral agencies. The supplier does not ship anything abroad, yet can still access GST refund: either supply without payment of tax where permitted, or pay tax and claim a refund of unutilised input credit. Because eligibility turns on the project notification and contract paperwork, manufacturers bidding for such projects should fix the refund route in the bid documentation, not after invoicing. The wider export framework sits in our [GST exports guide](/gst-exports) and [GST refund](/gst-refund) service.

## What Do PLI Schemes and Semiconductor Policy Offer?

Production-linked incentive schemes invert the older input-subsidy logic: they reward measured output — incremental sales over base-year levels, sometimes employment or investment milestones. Registration windows close, incentive caps bind, and claims stand or fall on audited production and eligibility records. Treat PLI revenue as conditional income in forecasts until certified.

The semiconductor policy direction runs alongside: central support for fabs, display fabs, packaging and test facilities, and design-linked incentives for chip design startups, with state packages stacking on top. Programme windows, outlay caps and eligibility are revised across tranches, so structure capital expenditure against the current notification — our [SEZ and STPI guide](/sez-stpi) covers the location-regime choices that pair with these incentives.

## How Should Energy and Carbon Compliance Be Planned?

Energy costs are tax items, not just operating costs. Renewable purchase obligations, energy-intensity targets under the perform-achieve-trade framework and the emerging carbon credit trading mechanism create measurement, verification and certificate-management duties for large energy consumers, while listed groups add sustainability reporting to the stack. Energy and emissions data are moving from voluntary ESG decks into regulated, auditable reporting — build the data layer now rather than retrofit it.

## What Tax Reliefs Apply to New Machinery?

Plant and machinery depreciates at 15% on written-down value in the normal block. Specified taxpayers setting up manufacturing or power-generation capacity can claim additional first-year depreciation of 15% on new machinery, subject to acquisition and put-to-use timing — but electing certain concessional corporate regimes forgoes it. Model both regimes before the first capital invoice; the election is largely one-way. Depreciation planning sits within our [income tax](/income-tax) practice.

## Which Compliance Deadlines Matter Most?

Monthly GST filings (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B) with annual GSTR-9, advance tax instalments, TDS on contractor and professional payments, payroll dues, and the audit cycle: statutory audit for every company, [tax audit](/tax-audit) under Section 44AB above Rs 1 crore turnover (Rs 10 crore where 95% or more of transactions are digital), and CARO reporting on inventory and assets. A structured [internal audit](/internal-audit) programme covering stores, scrap, capital work-in-progress and wastage usually pays for itself in detected leakage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can manufacturers claim GST input credit on machinery?

Yes, input tax credit on plant and machinery is generally available under Section 16, subject to registration, supplier invoice reporting in GSTR-2B and no blocked credit under Section 17(5). Credit on later sale or demolition is reversed under Section 18.

### What is a deemed export under GST?

Deemed exports are goods manufactured in India and supplied against notified projects or procurements, such as those funded by multilateral or bilateral agencies. The supplier may claim refund of the tax, either without payment of tax or as unutilised input credit.

### How do PLI scheme incentives work?

Production-linked incentive schemes reward incremental sales and, in some sectors, employment or investment milestones achieved over scheme years. Claims require audited data, registration before the window and continued compliance with scheme conditions, which change with each notification.

### Is extra depreciation available on new machinery?

Plant and machinery normally depreciates at 15% on written-down value. Specified taxpayers may claim additional first-year depreciation of 15% on new machinery, but concessional corporate regime elections can restrict it. Confirm your regime before modelling cash flow.

### Do semiconductor fabs get special incentives in India?

Yes. The semiconductor policy direction supports fabs, display fabs, packaging and test units and chip design with fiscal incentives under programme windows. Eligibility, caps and deadlines change with each tranche, so verify the current notification before committing capital.

### Which audits apply to a manufacturing company?

Statutory audit under the Companies Act 2013 is mandatory for companies. Tax audit under Section 44AB applies above turnover thresholds of Rs 1 crore, rising to Rs 10 crore where digital transactions are 95% or more. Internal audits add inventory and process control.
