> Tax and accounting explainer videos are short, focused recordings in which chartered accountants break down one compliance topic at a time — a GST return, an ITR form, a budget change — so that founders, finance teams and individuals can understand the rule and the required action in minutes rather than hours.

## What Our Tax and Accounting Explainer Videos Cover

The library is organised as a set of evergreen series rather than one-off uploads. Each recording targets a single question and pairs with a written guide on this site for the detail.

- **GST explainers** — registration workflow, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B walkthroughs, input tax credit conditions, e-invoicing and e-way bill basics; the written detail sits in our [GST updates](/blog/gst-updates) column.
- **Income tax explainers** — choosing between the old and new regimes, ITR form selection, reading Form 26AS and AIS, computing capital gains, and responding to intimation or demand notices.
- **Startup finance series** — DPIIT recognition, the section 80-IAC holiday in outline, fundraising documentation and ESOP fundamentals for first-time founders.
- **Payroll and compliance shorts** — TDS on salaries, the PF and ESI monthly cycle, and professional tax in plain terms.
- **Budget and policy webinars** — annual Union Budget walkthroughs and GST rate changes as they are announced, recorded as [budget analysis videos](/blog/budget-tax-updates) with business impact in focus.

## How to Use the Library with the Written Guides

Videos compress; guides carry the statutory references. Watch a recording for the concept, then use the matching [compliance guides](/guides) and the [FAQ hub](/faq) for thresholds, forms and deadlines. Nothing in the videos is a substitute for advice on your specific facts.

### Do these videos replace professional advice?
No. Videos explain rules in general terms, while applying them to specific facts needs a chartered accountant, since outcomes depend on documents, timing and sector.
