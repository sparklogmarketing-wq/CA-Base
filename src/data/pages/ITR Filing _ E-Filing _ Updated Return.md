> ITR filing is the process of disclosing your annual income — salary, business, capital gains and other sources — to the Income Tax Department through the official e-filing portal, claiming eligible deductions, paying balance tax, and verifying the return so it becomes valid under the Income-tax Act 1961.

## Which ITR Form Applies to You?

| Form | Who should use it |
|---|---|
| ITR-1 (Sahaj) | Resident individuals with total income up to Rs 50 lakh from salary, one house property and other sources such as interest |
| ITR-2 | Individuals and HUFs with capital gains, more than one house property, foreign income or assets, or directorships — no business income |
| ITR-3 | Individuals and HUFs earning business or professional income |
| ITR-4 (Sugam) | Residents opting for presumptive taxation under sections 44AD, 44ADA or 44AE, with income up to Rs 50 lakh |
| ITR-5 | Firms, LLPs, AOPs, BOIs, co-operative societies and local authorities |
| ITR-6 | Companies other than those claiming exemption under section 11 |
| ITR-7 | Persons required to file under sections 139(4A) to 139(4D) — trusts, political parties and similar institutions |

Filing the wrong form is a common trigger for defective-return notices under section 139(9), so we confirm the form before preparing the computation.

## ITR Filing Due Dates and the Cost of Missing Them

| Return type | Due date |
|---|---|
| Original ITR — non-audit taxpayers | 31 July of the assessment year |
| Original ITR — tax audit cases | 31 October of the assessment year |
| Belated return u/s 139(4) / revised return u/s 139(5) | 31 December of the assessment year |
| Updated return (ITR-U) u/s 139(8A) | Within 24 months from the end of the assessment year |

A late filing attracts interest under section 234A and a late fee under section 234F of up to Rs 5,000 (Rs 1,000 where total income does not exceed Rs 5 lakh). Losses, other than house-property loss, cannot be carried forward once the original due date lapses.

## Belated Returns and ITR-U: Filing After the Deadline

A belated return under section 139(4) preserves your compliance record for the year, though late fees and interest apply. A revised return under section 139(5) corrects an error or omission in a return already filed. For older, undisclosed positions, the updated return under section 139(8A) — ITR-U — allows filing within 24 months from the end of the relevant assessment year, subject to additional tax of 25% or 50% of the aggregate tax and interest depending on the delay, and subject to statutory restrictions (it cannot be used to declare nil or reduced income or to claim a refund).

## E-Verification Within 30 Days

Filing is complete only when the return is verified. E-verification must be done within 30 days of transmitting the return — via Aadhaar OTP, net banking, pre-validated bank or demat account EVC, or a digital signature. An unverified return is treated as not filed, and the original transmission date is lost. Our workflow includes e-verification on the day of filing, so this risk never arises for our clients.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which ITR form should I file?

It depends on your income heads. ITR-1 covers salaried residents up to Rs 50 lakh, ITR-2 applies where you have capital gains or foreign assets, ITR-3 for business income, and ITR-4 for presumptive taxation. We confirm the correct form before filing.

### What happens if I miss the ITR filing due date?

You can file a belated return under section 139(4) until 31 December with a late fee under section 234F of up to Rs 5,000, but losses other than house-property losses lapse and interest under section 234A applies.

### Can I file an updated return (ITR-U) for an earlier year?

Yes. Section 139(8A) lets you file an updated return within 24 months from the end of the assessment year, with additional tax of 25% or 50% of the tax and interest depending on the length of the delay.

### Is e-verification mandatory after filing?

Yes. A return must be e-verified within 30 days of transmission, using Aadhaar OTP, net banking or other electronic modes. An unverified return is treated as not filed, and the original filing date is lost.

### Do you file ITRs for businesses and companies?

Yes. We handle ITR-3, ITR-5 and ITR-6 filings, coordinate tax audit reports under section 44AB where applicable, and reconcile books, GST turnover and Form 26AS before submission.
