## How Company Registration in India Works: The SPICe+ Route

The SPICe+ web form consolidates name reservation, incorporation, DIN allotment and PAN/TAN into one workflow:

1. **DSC and name reservation** — Part A of SPICe+ proposes up to two names; the registry checks them against existing entities and trademarks.
2. **Part B filing** — subscriber and director details, share capital, registered office, and the e-MOA (INC-33) and e-AOA (INC-34).
3. **AGILE-PRO-S** — linked application for GSTIN, EPFO and ESIC registrations and, in several states including {state}, professional tax.
4. **Certificate of incorporation** — issued digitally with CIN, DINs for directors, and PAN/TAN.

For LLPs the equivalent route is name reservation through RUN-LLP followed by incorporation in Form FiLLiP, with the LLP agreement filed in Form 3 within thirty days. A foreign parent incorporating a subsidiary follows the same SPICe+ track with additional KYC — see our [subsidiary incorporation](/subsidiary-entity) service.

## Compliance That Follows Incorporation

Incorporation is the start, not the finish. New companies must appoint the first auditor within thirty days, file the INC-20A declaration of commencement of business, and obtain GST registration where turnover or inter-state supplies require it. Thereafter the annual cycle runs through AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-3 KYC and DPT-3 — our [ROC compliance](/roc-compliance) team manages this calendar so filings never lapse.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does company registration take in India?

Most private limited incorporations complete in roughly one to three weeks once documents are ready and the name is approved. Timelines depend on name availability and processing by the registry, so accurate documentation at the first filing matters.

### What documents are needed to register a company?

PAN and Aadhaar of each director, passport-size photographs, address proof, a registered office utility bill and owner NOC, plus Digital Signature Certificates. Foreign directors need passport and overseas address proof, apostilled or notarised where required.

### Can I use a residential address as the registered office?

Yes. A residential premises can serve as the registered office if you provide a recent utility bill and a no-objection certificate from the owner. Many early-stage startups in {city} begin this way and shift later.

### How many directors and shareholders are required?

A private limited company needs at least two directors and two shareholders; one person can hold both roles. A One Person Company needs only one member and nominee, while an LLP needs at least two designated partners.

### Is a Digital Signature Certificate mandatory?

Yes. All incorporation filings are made online on the registry portal, so every subscriber and witness must hold a valid DSC. We arrange Class 3 DSCs as part of the engagement.
