> GST return filing is the periodic reporting of outward supplies, inward supplies, tax collected and input tax credit by registered businesses through forms such as GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, reconciled against GSTR-2B. Timely, accurate filing keeps input tax credit flowing to buyers, avoids late fees and interest, and protects the business from departmental notices.

## Which GST Returns Does Your Business File?

| Return / form | What it reports | Frequency | Standard due date |
|---|---|---|---|
| GSTR-1 | Invoice-wise outward supplies | Monthly; quarterly under QRMP | 11th of the following month; 13th after the quarter under QRMP |
| IFF | Optional early upload of B2B invoices | First two months of each quarter (QRMP) | 13th of the following month |
| GSTR-3B | Self-assessed summary with tax payment | Monthly; quarterly under QRMP | 20th of the following month; 22nd or 24th after the quarter under QRMP |
| PMT-06 | Provisional tax payment | First two months of each quarter (QRMP) | 25th of the following month |
| CMP-08 | Composition scheme self-assessment | Quarterly | 18th after the quarter |
| GSTR-9 / GSTR-9C | Annual return; reconciliation where applicable | Annual | 31 December following the financial year |

## GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B — The Core Pair

GSTR-1 reports your outward supplies invoice by invoice and feeds your customers' GSTR-2B, so accuracy here protects their credit as much as yours. GSTR-3B is the summary return where liability is discharged; its figures must agree with GSTR-1 and with the books. Filing 3B before finalising GSTR-1, or carrying unreconciled credit forward month after month, is how small discrepancies become large demands — with interest at 18 percent per annum on short-paid tax.

## Monthly vs Quarterly: The QRMP Scheme

The QRMP scheme lets taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to Rs 5 crore file quarterly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B instead of monthly returns, paying a provisional 35 percent tax through PMT-06 in the first two months of the quarter. It reduces filing workload significantly, but B2B suppliers should weigh it against customer expectations: buyers on monthly credit cycles prefer vendors who upload invoices every month, which is exactly what the optional IFF provides.

## Annual Return GSTR-9 and Reconciliation GSTR-9C

The GSTR-9 annual return consolidates the year's GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B data and is due by 31 December after each financial year. GSTR-9C, the reconciliation statement between audited financial statements and the annual return, has historically applied to taxpayers above Rs 2 crore turnover, with its applicability adjusted by successive rule changes — we confirm the current-year requirement before advising. Remember the hard cutoff: credit not claimed by 30 November after the year end (or annual return filing, whichever is earlier) lapses under section 16(4).

## Reconciliation With GSTR-2B Protects Your ITC

Input tax credit is claimable only to the extent it reflects in your GSTR-2B. ## Late Fees, Interest and Clean Compliance

Missed GSTR-3B deadlines attract a late fee of Rs 50 per day (Rs 20 for nil returns), capped as prescribed, and 18 percent annual interest on unpaid tax. Repeated defaults also raise audit risk. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I miss a GSTR-3B due date?

A late fee of Rs 50 per day applies (Rs 20 for nil returns), capped as prescribed, plus interest at 18 percent per annum on unpaid tax. Late GSTR-1 filings also suppress the credit your customers can claim, damaging relationships.

### Should my business opt for the QRMP scheme?

If aggregate turnover is up to Rs 5 crore, QRMP cuts filings to one quarterly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B with a monthly PMT-06 payment. Choose monthly filing instead if your B2B customers need credit every month.

### Can a filed GST return be revised?

No. GST returns cannot be revised once filed. Errors are corrected through amendments in subsequent returns — invoices, credit notes and rate changes are adjusted in the table-level amendment facilities of GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B.

### What is GSTR-2B and why does it matter?

GSTR-2B is the auto-drafted statement of your inward supplies generated after the 14th of each month. Input tax credit is claimable only to the extent it appears in GSTR-2B, so vendor discipline directly controls your credit.

### When is the GST annual return due?

GSTR-9 for a financial year is due by 31 December of the following year. Input tax credit for a period must be claimed by 30 November after the year end or the filing of the annual return, whichever is earlier.
