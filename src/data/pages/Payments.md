> Online payment for CA services at {brand} covers professional fees for audit, tax, GST, payroll and advisory engagements. Clients can pay by NEFT, RTGS, UPI, cheque or card, and every payment is acknowledged with a GST-compliant invoice or fee receipt for accounting and TDS records.

## How to Make an Online Payment for CA Services at {brand}

Invoices from {brand} can be settled through any of the modes below. Bank transfers remain the most reliable route to pay CA fees online for engagement retainers; UPI and card links suit smaller or remote payments. Quote the invoice reference with your remittance so realisation is matched promptly.

| Payment mode | Best suited for | Reference needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NEFT / RTGS | engagement fees, annual retainers | invoice number, our bank account and IFSC as printed on the invoice |
| UPI | smaller invoices, quick clearances | UPI ID printed on the invoice |
| Cheque / demand draft | payments handed over at the office | payable in Bangalore, invoice number on the reverse |
| Card via secure link | remote clients, on request | link issued against a specific invoice |

## Invoices, GST and TDS

Professional fees are billed through GST-compliant invoices carrying our GSTIN and the applicable SAC classification, with GST charged at the prescribed rate. Where TDS applies, payments for professional services fall under section 194J of the Income-tax Act 1961 — deduct at the prescribed rate, deposit within the due date and share the challan details so the credit is reflected against our PAN in Form 26AS.

## Payment Terms and Receipts

Terms sit in your engagement letter, read with our [terms and conditions](/terms-and-conditions) — compliance retainers are usually billed periodically, while audit and advisory engagements may be invoiced in milestones. Every realisation is acknowledged with a receipt quoting the invoice number and payment reference; consolidated statements are available on request for your books and audit.

## Billing Queries and Refunds

Questions on an invoice go first to your engagement manager, or to {contact_email} with the invoice attached; we respond with a written explanation or a corrected invoice. Genuine overpayments are adjusted against the next invoice or returned through the original payment channel. Refund of completed statutory fees is governed by the engagement letter terms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What payment methods does {brand} accept?

NEFT and RTGS bank transfers, UPI, cheques and demand drafts, and card payments through a secure link issued on request. Our bank account details appear on every invoice.

### Do professional fees attract GST?

Yes. Fees for audit, tax and advisory services are charged plus GST at the applicable rate, and invoices carry our GSTIN and the correct SAC classification.

### Can I deduct TDS on your fees?

Where payments for professional services attract TDS under section 194J, clients may deduct tax at the prescribed rate and share the challan details so due credit is reflected against our PAN.

### When is payment due?

Payment terms are set in the engagement letter — commonly periodic billing for compliance retainers and milestone instalments for larger audit and advisory engagements.

### Will I receive a receipt?

Yes. Every realisation is acknowledged with a GST invoice or fee receipt quoting the invoice number, service period and payment reference for your records.
