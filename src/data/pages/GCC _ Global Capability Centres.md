> Global Capability Centres in India are offshore units of multinational groups that deliver engineering, IT, analytics, finance and support functions from Indian cities such as {city}. Their tax and compliance profile spans SEZ or STPI regimes, captive transfer pricing, expat payroll and cross-border reporting, so entity design and process discipline matter from day one.

## Why Do Groups Set Up Global Capability Centres in India?

Cost arbitrage opened the door, but the talent pool keeps it open. India offers deep engineering, finance and analytics talent at scale, a mature vendor ecosystem, English-language delivery and time-zone overlap that lets centres run follow-the-sun models. {city} in particular concentrates GCC talent, technology parks and supporting institutions, which is why {state} hosts one of the country's largest concentrations of captives across software, aerospace, fintech and biotech. The operating model is simple; the tax architecture around it is not — and that is where most centres either leak money or attract avoidable scrutiny.

## What Incentives Can a GCC Access?

Incentive planning works on two layers. At the state layer, policies for GCCs and technology companies have offered benefits such as rental assistance, skilling grants, and fast-tracked approvals — each subject to eligibility thresholds, tenure limits and employment conditions, so model the cash value before committing.

At the indirect-tax layer, the choice of location regime matters more than headline incentives. An SEZ unit can suit a fully export-oriented centre, and recent liberalisation of the SEZ framework has widened the activities that qualify, including data-centre style operations. IT and ITeS exporters can alternatively operate under the STPI framework. Each route carries export obligations, periodic reporting and exit levies, which our [SEZ and STPI guide](/sez-stpi) explains in detail. Incentives are the garnish; getting the entity, location and regime decision right is the meal.

## How Does Transfer Pricing Work for Captive Centres?

Most GCCs are captive support service providers: they bear limited market risk, deliver services to group affiliates, and expect to recover costs plus a routine return. That is exactly how tax authorities test them — typically under a transactional net margin method applying a cost-plus mark-up benchmarked against comparable Indian companies.

The recurring disputes are predictable. Contracts that promise full cost recovery but describe risk-bearing inconsistent with actual conduct invite authorities to impute an entrepreneur-level return. Under-resourced routines that never refresh benchmarks fall behind comparables. And head-office management fees, secondment charges and shared-platform costs each need defensible allocation keys. Robust annual documentation, Form 3CEB filings and, where the exposure justifies it, an advance pricing agreement close most of these gaps. Our [transfer pricing practice](/transfer-pricing) manages benchmarking studies and APA strategy for captive structures.

## What Are the Expat Taxation Touchpoints?

GCC launches usually begin with a rotation of seconded specialists. Each secondment triggers questions: does the individual cross the 182-day residency threshold in the financial year, is the salary for Indian workdays taxable here, who withholds and under which section, and does the home-country social security still apply. India's social-security totalisation agreements with several countries can exempt eligible secondees from local provident fund contributions, and treaty relief under the relevant DTAA can prevent double taxation — provided certificates of residence and withholding positions are documented before payroll runs. Where exemption claims fail, the employer ends up funding the shortfall.

|---|---|

| GST | Registration, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B monthly, GSTR-9 annually, LUT and refunds for export services |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Global Capability Centre?

A Global Capability Centre is a captive offshore unit of a multinational group, delivering IT, engineering, R&D, analytics, finance or shared-services work from India. {city} is among the largest GCC clusters in the country.

### Is SEZ registration mandatory for a GCC?

No. Many GCCs operate from the domestic tariff area under normal tax. An SEZ unit suits export-oriented operations seeking indirect-tax benefits, subject to export obligations and the scheme conditions applicable to that unit.

### How is a captive GCC tested for transfer pricing?

Captive support units are typically tested under a cost-plus method, benchmarking a routine margin on total cost. Documentation must show that contractual risk allocation matches actual conduct; otherwise authorities may impute an entrepreneur-level return.

### Are seconded expatriates taxable in India?

Foreign employees working in India can become tax residents after 182 days in a financial year. Salary for work performed here is taxable in India, with TDS under Section 192; treaty relief and social-security agreements may moderate the burden.

### What incentives are available to GCCs in {city}?

State policy support may include rental assistance, skilling grants and rate-linked benefits, each with eligibility conditions. SEZ or STPI frameworks add indirect-tax advantages for qualifying export operations. Confirm current positions before relying on any incentive.

### Which annual filings does a GCC entity face?

Company income-tax return with Form 3CEB where transfer pricing applies, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B monthly with GSTR-9 annually, AOC-4 and MGT-7 under the Companies Act, PF and ESI returns, the FEMA annual return, plus SEZ or STPI periodic reports.
