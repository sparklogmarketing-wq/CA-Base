> Audit quality and going concern together define whether financial statements deserve reliance: audit quality is the discipline of standards, scepticism and documentation behind an opinion, while going concern under SA 570 assesses whether an entity can realise its assets and discharge liabilities for at least twelve months after the balance-sheet date.

Audit opinions are only as strong as the framework behind them. Two developments raised that bar: the migration to risk-based quality management modelled on ISQM 1, and regulator-led scrutiny by the {audit_regulator} of how auditors document scepticism, scope and judgment. This {brand} column explains those frameworks.

## What This Audit Quality Briefing Covers

Our audit quality and going concern analysis tracks five recurring frameworks:

| Framework | What it governs | Practical effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SA 570 going concern | Continuity assessment by management and auditor | MUTC paragraphs, adverse opinions |
| SA 299 joint audits | Allocation of responsibility between joint auditors | Each firm answers for its scope |
| ISQM 1 / SQM standards | Firm-level, risk-based quality management | Monitoring and leadership accountability |
| NFRA quality review | Regulator examination of audit files | Documentation becomes the battleground |
| SA 701 key audit matters | Communicating the most significant audit issues | Richer listed-company auditor reports |

## Going Concern Under SA 570: The Twelve-Month Test

SA 570 obliges management to assess going concern for at least twelve months from the financial statements date, and obliges the auditor to challenge that assessment rather than receive it. Triggers include recurring losses, negative cash flows, covenant breaches and loss of a principal customer or licence. Where a material uncertainty exists and disclosure is adequate, the report carries a material-uncertainty-related-to-going-concern paragraph; without adequate disclosure, the opinion must be adverse. In stress cycles, audit quality is often visible in this paragraph.

## Joint Audits and SA 299: Who Answers for What

Joint audits remain common in Indian banking and insurance, and SA 299 frames responsibility: areas are divided between the joint auditors, each firm is accountable for its allocated areas, and the report is signed after joint discussion. Regulatory scrutiny — including {audit_regulator} reviews — tests each firm against its allocated scope. For audit committees, this translates into clear engagement letters, documented work allocation and joint quality review before signing.

## ISQM 1 and Risk-Based Quality Management

India has adopted the ISQM 1 architecture through ICAI's Standards on Quality Management, replacing the older checklist-driven regime. A firm must identify quality risks — acceptance, independence, resources, engagement performance, monitoring — and design responses, with accountability at leadership level. Two consequences reach clients directly: audits begin with sharper acceptance decisions, and monitoring findings feed back into engagement execution. A firm that declines an unmanageable engagement is demonstrating quality management, not weakness.

## NFRA Quality Review Direction and the Auditor's Report

The {audit_regulator}'s file reviews have become India's most consequential quality review mechanism for larger engagements. Recurrent themes — undocumented scepticism, over-reliance on management-produced information, gaps between risk assessment and procedures — read as a checklist for audit files. Auditor reporting has expanded alongside: key audit matters under SA 701 now disclose the judgments that mattered most in listed-company audits. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does SA 570 require on going concern?

SA 570 requires management to assess the entity's ability to continue as a going concern for at least twelve months from the balance-sheet date, and requires the auditor to evaluate that assessment, test the evidence and conclude on disclosure.

### What is a material uncertainty related to going concern?

A condition that may cast significant doubt on the entity's ability to continue — recurrent losses, covenant breaches, liquidity shortfalls. With adequate disclosure the auditor adds a material-uncertainty paragraph; without it, the opinion is adverse.

### How does SA 299 apply to joint audits?

Under SA 299, joint auditors divide audit areas between them, and each is responsible only for the areas allocated, while jointly discussing and signing the report. Regulators examine each firm against its allocated scope.

### What is ISQM 1 and how is India adopting it?

ISQM 1 is the global standard for firm-level quality management, adopted in India through ICAI's Standards on Quality Management. It replaces checklists with a risk-based system covering acceptance, resources, performance and monitoring.

### What are key audit matters in the auditor's report?

Key audit matters are the matters most significant in the audit — revenue recognition, valuation of complex instruments, litigation provisions. SA 701 requires them to be described in listed-company auditor reports.

### How does NFRA oversight affect audit quality?

NFRA quality reviews read the working paper file as the primary evidence of quality. Firms now document scepticism, scope decisions and judgments contemporaneously, and boards should expect longer evidence checklists.
