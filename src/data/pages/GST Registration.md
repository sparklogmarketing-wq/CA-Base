> GST registration is the process by which a business obtains a Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) under the CGST Act, 2017, becoming a recognised taxable person entitled to issue tax invoices and claim input tax credit on purchases — mandatory once turnover crosses the prescribed threshold or in specified categories of supply.

## Who Needs GST Registration? Thresholds and Compulsory Cases

Registration is based on aggregate turnover computed pan-India for the financial year, but the threshold differs by the nature of supply and the state:

| Category of supplier | Registration threshold |
|---|---|
| Suppliers of goods (normal category states, including Karnataka) | Rs 40 lakh aggregate turnover |
| Service providers (normal category states) | Rs 20 lakh aggregate turnover |
| Special category states — suppliers of goods | Rs 20 lakh aggregate turnover |
| Special category states — service providers | Rs 10 lakh aggregate turnover |

Beyond the thresholds, registration is compulsory irrespective of turnover for:

- Anyone making inter-state outward supplies of goods.
- Casual taxable persons and non-resident taxable persons.
- Persons liable to pay tax under reverse charge.
- Agents of a supplier and Input Service Distributors.
- Persons supplying through e-commerce platforms where the platform or supplier is liable to tax.
- Businesses migrating from earlier law or voluntarily opting in — voluntary registration is always permitted and is often chosen by B2B startups whose customers demand a GSTIN.

Small suppliers may alternatively evaluate the composition scheme — broadly up to Rs 1.5 crore turnover at 1 percent for traders and manufacturers, 5 percent for restaurants, with a 6 percent service composition up to Rs 50 lakh — though composition taxpayers cannot collect tax from customers or pass on credit.

## Documents Required for GST Registration

| Entity type | Key documents |
|---|---|
| Proprietorship | PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor, photograph, bank proof (cancelled cheque or statement), principal place of business proof |
| Partnership / LLP | Firm PAN, partnership deed or LLP agreement, certificate of registration, partners' PAN and Aadhaar, authorised signatory details, bank proof |
| Private / public limited company | Company PAN, certificate of incorporation, MOA and AOA, directors' DIN and Aadhaar, digital signature, board resolution authorising the signatory, bank proof |
| All entities | Proof of principal place of business: electricity bill, and rent agreement with owner NOC or property tax receipt for rented premises |

Names must match PAN records exactly; mismatches are the most common cause of REG-03 clarifications.

## CGST, SGST and IGST — How the Tax Splits

A single GSTIN lets you charge the right components automatically: intra-state supplies attract CGST plus SGST (or UTGST in union territories), while inter-state supplies and imports attract IGST. After the September 2025 rationalisation, the rate structure is built around two principal slabs of 5 percent and 18 percent, with a 40 percent demerit rate for specified goods and concessional or nil rates for essentials — so correct HSN classification from day one decides what your invoices carry.

## How to Apply for GST Registration in Bangalore: Step-by-Step Process

1. **Part A:** Generate a TRN on the GST portal using PAN, mobile number and email.
2. **Part B:** Complete business details, HSN codes, place of business, bank details and authorised signatory, and upload documents.
3. **Aadhaar authentication:** Promoters complete Aadhaar authentication where applicable — the fastest route to approval.
4. **Officer scrutiny:** If the proper officer needs more information, a notice in FORM GST REG-03 is issued; you must respond in FORM REG-04 within seven days.
5. **Approval:** On satisfaction, the certificate of registration in FORM GST REG-06 with your 15-digit GSTIN is issued, normally within seven working days of a clean application.

The certificate takes effect from the date liability arose if you apply within 30 days of becoming liable.

## Penalties for Operating Without a GSTIN

Operating without required registration exposes you to the tax due, interest at 18 percent per annum and a penalty of Rs 10,000 or the tax involved, whichever is higher. You also cannot issue tax invoices, and customers lose credit for the unregistered period. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does GST registration take?

A clean, Aadhaar-authenticated application is typically approved within about seven working days of the ARN. If the officer issues a REG-03 clarification, the clock pauses until you respond, so document quality decides the timeline.

### What is the turnover limit for GST registration?

Rs 40 lakh aggregate turnover for suppliers of goods and Rs 20 lakh for service providers in normal states like Karnataka. Special category states have lower limits of Rs 20 lakh and Rs 10 lakh. Some supplies require registration irrespective of turnover.

### Is Aadhaar authentication mandatory for GST registration?

It is not mandatory, but applications without Aadhaar authentication face document verification or a physical visit to the place of business, which slows approval. Aadhaar authentication is the fastest route to a GSTIN.

### Can I get GST registration using a residential address?

Yes. The principal place of business can be a rented or owned residential premises, supported by an electricity bill as ownership proof plus a rent agreement with the owner's NOC or property tax receipt.

### What is the penalty for not registering under GST?

Operating without a required GSTIN attracts the tax due, interest at 18 percent per annum and a penalty of Rs 10,000 or the tax involved, whichever is higher. You also cannot issue tax invoices or transfer credit for that period.
