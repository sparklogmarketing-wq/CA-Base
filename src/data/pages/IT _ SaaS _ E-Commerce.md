> Taxation of IT, SaaS and e-commerce companies in India spans GST on digital and OIDAR supplies, export-of-service classification, tax collection at source by e-commerce operators, Ind AS 115 revenue recognition and ESOP payroll tax. Aligning each layer protects input credit, cash flow and audit readiness.

## Why Is Taxation of IT, SaaS and E-Commerce Companies in India Different?

A digital business can serve a global customer base from one office — exactly what Indian tax law finds interesting. The same subscription invoice may be a domestic service, an inter-state online supply or a zero-rated export depending on where the customer sits and how payment arrives. Platforms add obligations that sit with the marketplace, not the seller. This guide maps the rules that decide how digital revenue is taxed.

## How Does GST Apply to OIDAR and Digital Services?

Section 2(48) of the CGST Act defines OIDAR services as those delivered over the internet or an electronic network — essentially automated, minimal human intervention, impossible without the technology. Cloud hosting, SaaS subscriptions, online advertising, streaming and downloadable software sit in this basket.

| Scenario | GST treatment |
|---|---|
| Domestic software subscription | 18% GST charged; recipient claims ITC under Section 16 |
| Inter-state online supply to a registered recipient | IGST charged by the supplier |
| OIDAR supply from overseas to unregistered Indian recipients | Overseas operator registers via the simplified OIDAR route and pays tax |
| Supply from India meeting export conditions | Zero-rated; bill under LUT and refund input credit |

Under the post-September 2025 structure of 5% and 18% slabs plus a 40% demerit rate, software and IT services continue broadly at 18%. Document the service, goods-licence or composite-supply classification before the first invoice — the [GST services](/gst-services) hub covers registration and rates.

## When Does a Software or IT Supply Qualify as an Export of Services?

Section 2(6) of the IGST Act requires five conditions to be satisfied together:

1. The supplier is in India.
2. The recipient is outside India.
3. Place of supply is outside India.
4. Payment arrives in convertible foreign exchange — or rupees where specifically permitted.
5. The supplier and recipient are not merely establishments of a distinct person.

Where all five are met, the supply is zero-rated under Section 16 of the IGST Act: bill under a bond or Letter of Undertaking and claim a refund of unutilised input credit, or pay IGST and reclaim it. Remote development for a foreign parent needs care: condition five treats branches of one legal entity as one person, and group payment routing must still pass the foreign-exchange test.

## What Do E-Commerce Operators Owe Under GST?

| Obligation | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Tax collected at source, Section 52 | Operator collects TCS at the prescribed rate on net taxable supplies through the platform and reports it in GSTR-8; suppliers transfer the amount into their cash ledger |
| Liability under Section 9(5) | For notified services such as passenger transport, restaurant and housekeeping booked through the platform, the operator — not the service provider — is liable to pay GST |

Sellers should reconcile GSTR-8 TCS before crediting their ledger — mismatches block credit utilisation. Mechanics sit in our [GST TDS, TCS and e-commerce guide](/gst-tds-tcs-ecommerce).

## How Is SaaS Revenue Recognised Under Ind AS 115?

Ind AS 115 applies a five-step model: identify the contract, the performance obligations, the transaction price, allocate it, and recognise revenue as each obligation is satisfied. Subscriptions are recognised over the term, with upfront amounts parked as contract liabilities until service is delivered; implementation fees, usage pricing and free-trial concessions each need a documented conclusion. For companies taxed under Sections 115BAA or 115BAB, taxable income starts from book profit with limited adjustments, so revenue timing flows straight into tax. Our [Ind AS guide](/ind-as-accounting-standards) covers the standard in depth.

## How Are ESOPs and Tech Payroll Taxed?

Stock options are taxable at exercise: fair market value on the specified date minus the exercise price is a perquisite under Section 17(2), taxed as salary, with TDS under Section 192. For eligible-startup employees, Section 192(1C) defers that TDS — payable within fourteen days of the earliest of forty-eight months from the end of the relevant assessment year, the employee's exit, or share sale. Pair every grant with [payroll tracking](/payroll-services) alongside Form 16 and 24Q.

## What About R&D Deduction and Section 43B(h)?

Section 35(2AB) allows a weighted deduction for approved in-house R&D facilities, subject to sector eligibility, DSIR approval and notified sunset dates — verify the current position before claiming.

Section 43B(h) changes vendor economics: payments to micro and small enterprise suppliers are deductible only on actual payment once they cross the MSMED Act credit period. Businesses paying large vendor networks should capture MSME status at onboarding and monitor cycles against those timelines. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are cloud and SaaS services taxable under GST?

Yes. Software subscriptions, hosting and platform services are taxable supplies, generally at 18%. Supplied from India to overseas recipients who meet the export conditions, they are zero-rated exports and unutilised input credit is refundable.

### What are the five export of services conditions?

Supplier in India; recipient outside India; place of supply outside India; payment in convertible foreign exchange (or rupees where permitted); and supplier and recipient not merely establishments of a distinct person. All five must be met together.

### Who collects GST TCS on an online marketplace?

The e-commerce operator collects tax at source under Section 52 on net taxable supplies facilitated through the platform, deposits it and reports it in GSTR-8. Suppliers then claim the amount into their electronic cash ledger.

### How is an ESOP perquisite taxed?

Fair market value on the specified date minus the exercise price is taxed as salary under Section 17(2). The employer deducts TDS under Section 192; eligible startup employees may defer TDS under Section 192(1C).

### What does Section 43B(h) mean for platforms?

Payments to micro and small enterprise vendors are deductible on actual payment once beyond the MSME Act credit period. Platforms and outsourcing firms should track vendor status and payment cycles accordingly.

### Is the Section 35(2AB) R&D deduction still available?

Yes, subject to eligibility. The weighted deduction for approved in-house R&D facilities depends on sector coverage, DSIR approval and notified sunset dates. Confirm the current position before claiming.
