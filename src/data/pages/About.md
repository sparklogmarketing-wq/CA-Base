## Who We Are — Chartered Accountants in Bangalore

{brand} is a chartered accountancy firm based in Bangalore, Karnataka. We act for private limited companies, LLPs, partnership firms, startups, trusts and individuals — including NRIs with Indian income — on a portfolio that runs from a single income tax return to a fully outsourced finance function. Whatever the size of the engagement, the working method is constant: scope agreed in writing, work reviewed by a qualified chartered accountant, and filings completed ahead of statutory due dates.

## What We Do

- **Audit and assurance** — statutory audits under the Companies Act 2013, tax audits under section 44AB of the Income-tax Act 1961, internal audit and due diligence support through our [statutory audit](/statutory-audit) practice.
- **Direct tax** — [income tax services](/income-tax) covering ITR filing, tax planning, advance tax, TDS compliance and representation in assessments and notices.
- **Indirect tax** — [GST services](/gst-services) covering registration, monthly returns, annual GSTR-9, input tax credit reconciliation and notice handling.
- **Corporate and outsourced services** — ROC compliance, payroll processing, bookkeeping, virtual CFO support and entity registration for new ventures.

## How We Work

Engagements open with a discovery call and a written engagement letter covering deliverables, timelines and fees.
