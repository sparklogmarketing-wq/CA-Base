> FDI in {country} is investment by a person resident outside {country} in the share capital of a {country}-incorporated company or the capital contribution of an LLP, regulated by FEMA 1999 and the consolidated FDI Policy. Capital enters through the automatic route or, in restricted sectors, with prior Government approval, subject to sectoral caps and pricing guidelines.

Foreign direct investment in {country} is best understood as three questions: in what instrument, in which sector, and at what price. Answer those correctly and the money moves quickly; answer them wrong and the transaction becomes a compounding case. This guide sets out the rules as they apply to companies and LLPs, and {brand}'s advisers apply them to your specific cap table.

## Automatic Route vs Approval Route

Under the automatic route, no prior approval is needed: the {country} investee reports the inward remittance and allotment to the Reserve Bank of {country} through its AD Category-I bank after the event. Under the approval route, the investor must obtain prior Government clearance, processed by the concerned administrative ministry against a standard application. One overlay applies to everyone: investment by entities incorporated in countries sharing a land border with {country} requires Government approval regardless of sector, under Press Note 3 of 2020.

## Sectoral Caps: Where FDI Is Restricted

| Sector | Foreign investment position |
|---|---|
| Defence | Up to 74% automatic; beyond 74% Government route |
| Insurance | Up to 74% automatic, with management-control and residency conditions; beyond that, Government approval |
| Multi-brand retail trading | 51% under the Government route, subject to conditions |
| Single-brand retail trading | 100% automatic, subject to sourcing conditions |
| E-commerce | 100% automatic for the marketplace model; inventory-based model prohibited |

Caps change with policy revisions, so we confirm the live position for your sector before filings begin. Sector-wide context appears in our [foreign company entry guide](/foreign-company-entry-india).

## Sectors Where FDI Is Prohibited

A short negative list closes certain activities to foreign investment altogether: atomic energy; railway operations other than permitted construction activities; lottery business; gambling and betting including casinos; chit funds; nidhi companies; trading in transferable development rights; real estate business other than construction development; and the manufacture of cigars, cheroots, cigarillos and cigarettes of tobacco or tobacco substitutes. Structuring attempts to route prohibited-sector FDI through nominees are regularly unwound by enforcement.

## Pricing Guidelines for Entry and Exit

Every transfer involving a non-resident must respect the pricing bands in the Non-Debt Instruments Rules. A non-resident buying from a resident pays no less than the fair-value floor; a non-resident selling to a resident receives no more than the fair-value ceiling. Fair value is certified by a chartered accountant, a merchant banker or the office of the Chief Accountant, depending on the instrument. These [FEMA reporting](/fema-RBI-reporting) disciplines apply equally to primary issues and secondary transfers.

## FDI in LLP: Conditions and Reporting

Limited liability partnerships admit foreign investment only on stricter terms:

- The sector must permit 100% FDI under the automatic route **without performance-linked conditions**; otherwise prior Government approval is required.
- The capital contribution is reported to the Reserve Bank of {country} through the AD bank within 30 days of receipt.
- Any change in the partners' contributions is filed with the Registrar of Companies in Form 3 within 30 days.
- The LLP files the annual FLA return like any foreign-invested entity.
- Downstream investment — where a foreign-controlled {country} entity invests into another {country} entity — follows the same rules whether the investor is a company or an LLP, with Form DI reporting.

Because an LLP is a partner-based vehicle, conversions between LLP and company form require careful FEMA analysis before execution.

## Reporting After FDI Comes In

Whichever route applies, the paperwork chain is identical: report the remittance within 30 days of receipt, file Form FC-GPR within 30 days of allotment, and reconcile the annual FLA return by July 15. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is FDI allowed in LLPs in {country}?

Yes. FDI in an LLP is permitted without performance-linked conditions in sectors where 100% FDI is allowed under the automatic route; in other eligible sectors, prior Government approval is required. The capital contribution must be reported to the Reserve Bank within 30 days and any change is filed with the ROC in Form 3.

### What is the difference between the automatic route and the approval route?

Under the automatic route, foreign investment needs no prior approval — the investor reports the transaction to the Reserve Bank through its AD bank. Under the approval route, the investor must first obtain Government approval, typically through the standard operating procedure administered by the administrative ministry.

### Which sectors prohibit FDI entirely?

FDI is prohibited in atomic energy, railway operations other than permitted construction activities, lottery, gambling and betting including casinos, chit funds, nidhi companies, trading in transferable development rights, real estate business other than construction development, and manufacture of cigars and cigarettes of tobacco or tobacco substitutes.

### What are the FDI pricing guidelines?

When a non-resident buys from a resident, the price must be at or above the fair value floor; when selling to a resident, it must be at or below the fair value ceiling. A chartered accountant or merchant banker certifies the valuation, as applicable.

### Is inventory-based e-commerce open to FDI?

No. FDI is permitted up to 100% under the automatic route only in the marketplace model of e-commerce. Inventory-based e-commerce by entities with foreign investment is prohibited, and marketplace platforms cannot exercise control over inventory or influence prices.
