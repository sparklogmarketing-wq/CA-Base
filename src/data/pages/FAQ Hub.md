## CA Services FAQs: What a Chartered Accountancy Firm Can Do

A chartered accountancy firm does far more than sign audit reports. Typical engagements include bookkeeping, tax compliance, statutory and internal audits, payroll and virtual CFO support. The CA services FAQs we hear most often concern scope — what a firm can legitimately take off the owner's desk.

**Can a CA firm handle accounting and bookkeeping?** Yes. Businesses routinely outsource their books so that compliance-ready records, monthly management accounts and reconciliations are maintained by qualified professionals under supervision.

**Can a CA firm help with tax planning?** Yes — legitimate planning arranges affairs within the four corners of the Income-tax Act 1961: the right presumptive scheme, well-timed deductions, correctly structured transactions. It never means concealing income.

**Can a CA firm advise on cash flow, profit improvement and fundraising?** Yes. Through [virtual CFO services](/cfo-services), we build cash flow forecasts, analyse margins and prepare lender- and investor-ready financials for loans or equity rounds.

**Can a CA firm help detect fraud and strengthen internal controls?** Yes. A structured internal audit reviews transactions, tests controls, flags anomalies and helps design internal financial controls proportionate to the business.

## GST Questions

**Who must register for GST?** Registration becomes mandatory once aggregate turnover crosses Rs 40 lakh for goods or Rs 20 lakh for services (lower in special category states), and for interstate suppliers and e-commerce sellers. See [GST registration](/gst-registration) for documents and process.

**Which GST returns does a regular taxpayer file?** GSTR-1 for outward supplies and GSTR-3B as the summary return with payment, monthly or quarterly under QRMP, plus annual GSTR-9. Our [GST return filing](/gst-return-filing) page sets out the cycle.

**Why do input tax credits get rejected?** Most ITC failures trace to section 16(2) — the supplier has not filed returns, the invoice is not in your name, or payment exceeds 180 days.

## Income Tax Questions

**Which ITR form applies to me?** It depends on your income heads: ITR-1 for salary and one house property with total income up to Rs 50 lakh, ITR-2 for capital gains, ITR-3 for business income and ITR-4 for presumptive taxation. The [ITR filing](/itr-filing) page explains the selection.

**What are the ITR due dates?** Broadly July 31 for non-audit taxpayers and October 31 where a tax audit applies, subject to extensions notified by the {direct_tax_authority}.

**I received an income tax notice — what now?** Do not ignore it. Identify the section, compare figures with Form 26AS and your AIS, and respond within the stated window. Our [scrutiny and notices](/income-tax-scrutiny-notices) team handles drafting and representation.

## Audit and Assurance Questions

**Is a statutory audit compulsory for a private limited company?** Yes. Every private company is audited annually under the Companies Act 2013, irrespective of turnover — see [statutory audit](/statutory-audit) for scope and process.

**When does a tax audit under section 44AB apply?** Where turnover exceeds Rs 1 crore, relaxed to Rs 10 crore where at least 95 per cent of receipts and payments are digital. Details are in our [tax audit](/tax-audit) guide.

## Company Registration and Compliance Questions

**Which entity should a new business choose?** A private limited company suits ventures planning to raise equity; an LLP suits professional and family-run firms; a proprietorship suits very small operations. The choice shapes taxation, compliance load and fundraising ability.

**What annual filings does a private company face?** AOC-4 for financial statements, MGT-7 for the annual return, DIR-3 KYC for directors and DPT-3, apart from income tax and GST returns. The [ROC compliance](/roc-compliance) page lists the full calendar.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a CA firm provide accounting and bookkeeping services?
Yes. Firms maintain books, monthly management accounts, reconciliations and payroll under supervised engagements, so records stay compliance-ready and owners receive reliable numbers for decisions.

### Which documents are required for GST registration?
PAN and Aadhaar of promoters, proof of principal place of business (ownership documents or rent agreement with utility bill), bank details and photographs, plus entity-specific annexures such as partnership deeds or board resolutions.

### What is the difference between statutory audit and tax audit?
A statutory audit under the Companies Act 2013 examines a company's financial statements and applies to every private company. A tax audit under section 44AB of the Income-tax Act 1961 tests books and tax compliance above prescribed turnover limits.

### When are income tax return due dates in India?
Generally July 31 for taxpayers not subject to audit and October 31 where an audit applies, subject to government extensions. Missing the date invites late fees and interest, so calendar discipline matters.

### Can a CA firm help respond to tax or GST notices?
Yes. The work involves identifying the demand, compiling reconciliations and evidence, drafting a reasoned reply and representing the client through proceedings, including faceless assessment and GST scrutiny stages.
