> The learning hub is the curated index of this site's knowledge base: ten learning pillars, each clustering related guides into a single reading path, covering business setup, company law compliance, income tax, international and NRI taxation, GST, audit and assurance, the outsourced finance function, India market entry, industry-specific rules and city-level service delivery.

Most compliance content fails readers by organisation: everything is published, nothing is sequenced. This hub fixes that. Every guide on the site sits in exactly one of the ten pillars below, ordered so each shelf reads from foundations to specifics.

## Learning Pillars

1. **[Starting a Business](/learn/starting-a-business)** — structure comparisons and registration walkthroughs for companies, LLPs, OPCs and partnerships, plus DPIIT, Udyam and trust registrations.
2. **[Company Law & ROC Compliance](/learn/company-law-compliance)** — the ROC filing calendar, the MCA form map, director KYC and beneficial ownership, CSR, dematerialisation and closure routes.
3. **[Income Tax & Compliance](/learn/income-tax-compliance)** — ITR selection and filing, capital gains, presumptive schemes, scrutiny notices, unexplained credits and dispute resolution.
4. **[International & NRI Tax](/learn/international-nri-tax)** — transfer pricing, NRI asset strategy, DTAA and foreign tax credits, and foreign subsidiary planning.
5. **[GST](/learn/gst)** — registration, the return cycle, input credit, e-invoicing, refunds, audits, TDS and the sector and rate trackers.
6. **[Audit & Assurance](/learn/audit-assurance)** — statutory, internal, tax, IT and stock audits, public sector engagements and due diligence.
7. **[CFO & Finance Function](/learn/cfo-finance-function)** — accounting outsourcing, payroll and labour law, MIS and cost control, valuations, funding, restructuring and IPO readiness.
8. **[Doing Business in India](/learn/doing-business-in-india)** — FDI routes and sectoral caps, subsidiary, branch and liaison setup, FEMA and RBI reporting.
9. **[Tax & Compliance by Industry](/learn/by-industry)** — IT and SaaS, manufacturing and semiconductors, real estate, fintech and VDA, GCCs, and the SEZ-STPI regime.
10. **[CA Services in Your City](/learn/local-services)** — how each service runs on the ground in your city, from registrations to assessments.

## How the Pillars Are Built

The clustering is structural, not cosmetic: each page belongs to exactly one pillar, and the mapping is validated when the site builds, so guides cannot drift between shelves or go missing. Service pages, contact routes and utility pages keep their existing addresses — the pillars are an additional topic-first path through the same content. For quick answers, the [FAQ hub](/faq) indexes the site's questions, and the [forms and checklists reference](/forms-checklists) lists statutory formats by regulator.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Learning Hub?

A single shelf that groups every guide on this site into ten learning pillars, from starting a business to industry-specific compliance. Each pillar page lists its guides in reading order, so you can move from basics to specifics without leaving the shelf.

### How is the content organised?

Every content page on the site belongs to exactly one pillar — setup, company law, income tax, international and NRI tax, GST, audit, CFO and finance, India entry, industry, or city services. Utility pages such as contact, FAQ and payments stay outside the pillars.

### Where should a first-time founder start?

The Starting a Business pillar: structure comparisons first, then the registration walkthrough for the chosen entity, then the Company Law pillar for the compliance calendar that follows incorporation.

### Are these guides different from the service pages?

No — they are the same pages, regrouped for learning. Service and contact routes stay unchanged; the pillars add a topic-first path through the same content, useful when researching rather than engaging.

### How current is the content?

Guides are written against the law in force and refreshed as rules change; new guides slot into their pillar automatically at each build. Where numbers are threshold-dependent — limits, due dates, fee slabs — the guide states the position and its source rule.
