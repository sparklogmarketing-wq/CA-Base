## GST Advisory Services: Classification, Rates and Contracts

Advisory is where GST is won or lost. Since the September 2025 rationalisation, the rate map runs on two principal slabs of 5 percent and 18 percent with a 40 percent demerit rate for specified goods — which makes [GST rate classification](/gst-rates-notifications) a genuine judgment area at slab boundaries. - Exemption and composition eligibility checks before structures harden.

## Scrutiny Under Section 61: The ASMT-10 Scrutiny Notice

An ASMT-10 notice means the officer's data tools flagged your returns: GSTR-1 not matching 3B, credit exceeding 2B, negative liability or HSN anomalies. You get thirty days to explain through an ASMT-11 reply, supported by reconciliations; a convincing reply ends the matter in ASMT-12, while silence or a careless one converts scrutiny into demand proceedings. Our replies are built from your own data trails, which is why they hold up.

## Show Cause Notices and Demand Orders

The demand machinery runs on forms: a prior intimation (DRC-01A) or a formal [DRC-01 show cause notice](/gst-advisory-notices), your reply in DRC-06, a personal hearing under section 75 on request, the demand order in DRC-07 and its summary in DRC-08. Time limits protect you, so they must be tracked precisely:

| Tax period | Regime | Show cause notice window |
|---|---|---|
| Up to FY 2023-24, no fraud alleged | Section 73 | Three years from the annual return due date |
| Up to FY 2023-24, fraud or suppression alleged | Section 74 | Five years |
| FY 2024-25 onwards, general | Section 74A | Forty-two months from the annual return due date |
| FY 2024-25 onwards, suppression alleged | Section 74A | Fifty-four months |

A GST notice reply from our desk does three things: tests the notice against limitation, reconciles the numbers it relies on, and concedes only what the data genuinely supports — often with interest, avoiding penalty exposure entirely.

## Detention, Search, Summons and Registration Issues

Movement-side disputes — goods detained for e-way bill defects — proceed under section 129 through MOV-series notices and require fast, factual engagement to release consignments. Inspection and search powers under section 67 and summons under section 70 demand coordinated responses: attend, answer from records, never speculate. Registration cancellation notices (REG-17) and revocation applications (REG-21) are handled with the same discipline, because losing a GSTIN mid-year disrupts every customer relationship.

## Appeals and Escalation

Where the DRC-07 order is adverse, the [GST appeal under section 107](/gst-advisory-notices) lies before the appellate authority within three months, supported by a 10 percent pre-deposit of the tax in dispute, with one further month available on cause shown. Revisional powers under section 108 and the tribunal route under section 112 sit above it. Appeals are argued on the record created in the reply stage — one more reason notice replies are drafted with appeal in mind. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do on receiving a DRC-01 show cause notice?

Do not ignore it. Check the limitation date, reconcile the alleged demand against your returns and 2B data, file a reply in DRC-06 within the time given — usually thirty days — and request a personal hearing under section 75 where facts need arguing.

### How is section 74A different from sections 73 and 74?

For tax periods from FY 2024-25, section 74A subsumes the old sections 73 and 74 into one framework: show cause notices generally within 42 months of the annual return due date, or 54 months where fraud or suppression is alleged.

### Can a GST notice be closed by paying the tax?

Yes, at the intimation stage. Where tax and interest pointed out in a DRC-02 or DRC-03 intimation are paid, no show cause notice issues for the accepted amount. Paying correctly early is almost always cheaper than litigating late.

### What is the pre-deposit for filing a GST appeal?

An appeal under section 107 must be filed within three months of the DRC-07 order, with a pre-deposit of 10 percent of the tax under dispute. One further month is available on sufficient cause through condonation.

### Do I need a chartered accountant to reply to a GST notice?

Legally no, but replies create records that govern later appeals. A CA identifies limitation defects, reconciles data the officer relied on and drafts the reply so that admissions are not made under time pressure.
