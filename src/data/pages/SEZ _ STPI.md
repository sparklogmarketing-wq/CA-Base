> SEZ and STPI benefits in India combine indirect-tax relief — zero-rated supplies, input credit refunds and, for SEZ units, exemption on most domestic tariff area levies — with direct-tax holidays under Section 10AA for units notified before the 31 March 2021 cut-off. Registration, export obligations and periodic reporting decide whether benefits survive audit.

## What Are SEZ and STPI Benefits in India?

Both frameworks reward exporters, but differently. The SEZ regime under the SEZ Act 2005 demarcates physical territory: a unit inside the zone is treated, for customs and excise purposes, as outside India, unlocking duty exemptions on capital goods and inputs alongside GST zero-rating and, historically, a direct-tax holiday. STPI is a lighter registration route for software and IT-enabled exporters — no demarcated land, indirect-tax-led benefits. Choosing is a business-model decision: a software exporter in a rented office rarely needs an SEZ unit; a hardware manufacturer or large captive campus often does.

## What Does an SEZ Unit Get Under the SEZ Act 2005?

| Benefit | How it works |
|---|---|
| Zero-rated GST supplies | Supplies to the unit for authorised operations are zero-rated under Section 16 of the IGST Act; supplier bills under LUT and refunds unutilised input credit, or pays and claims refund |
| Duty-free procurement | Exemptions on customs duty for imports and on specified domestic procurements of capital goods and inputs for authorised operations |
| Direct-tax holiday | Section 10AA deduction over a 15-year schedule — 100% for the first five years, 50% for the next five, and a further 50% for five years where export profits are reserved — for units commencing operations on or before 31 March 2021 |
| DTA sales | Domestic sales permitted within limits, subject to the duties and levies applicable to such sales |
| Simplified regime | Single-window approvals, self-assessment and periodic performance reporting to the Development Commissioner |

Section 10AA deserves emphasis: the direct-tax leg is scheme-dependent and effectively closed for units commencing after 31 March 2021, so a new unit should be justified on indirect-tax and operational benefits alone — any holiday claim for a legacy unit is a technical exercise requiring the original approval papers.

## What Is the STPI Route for IT and ITeS Exporters?

Software technology parks registration suits exporters of software development, BPO, KPO and related IT-enabled services. The entity registers with STPI, files projected export performance, then sustains a reporting rhythm — billing and export statements through the year and an annual performance report evidencing realisation. Qualifying exports ride the indirect-tax relief routes all exporters use, including zero-rated billing and credit refunds. STPI carries no demarcated-land overhead and no direct-tax holiday; its value is administrative — a recognised export vehicle that banks, clients and tax authorities understand. For tech exporters weighing routes, our [IT, SaaS and e-commerce guide](/it-saas-ecommerce) covers the service-export conditions that apply regardless of registration.

## What Are Export Obligations and Reporting Duties?

Benefits are earned continuously, not granted once. SEZ units must maintain a positive net foreign exchange earning position over the block period fixed in the approval letter and file periodic reports with the Development Commissioner; STPI units track billed exports against projections in the annual performance report. Shortfalls invite penalties, exit levies or, in extremis, closure — so model export realisation quarterly and flag slippage before reports fall due. Realisation timing, advance remittances and inter-company settlements all move the NFE needle, which is why captives should align treasury flows with the obligation calendar; the structuring angle sits in our [Global Capability Centres guide](/gcc-global-capability-centres).

## How Do GST Refunds Work for SEZ Supplies?

Supplies to SEZ units and developers for authorised operations are zero-rated, so the supplier's money sits in input credit until refunded. Two routes exist: bill without payment of tax under a bond or LUT and claim refund of unutilised credit, or charge IGST and seek a refund. Both require the recipient's SEZ status and authorised-operation purpose to be documented, and refunds move only as fast as invoice data, GSTR filings and bank realisation certificates allow. Duty-free physical procurement runs through separate customs documentation. ## What Should a New Unit Plan For Today?

For units established now, the calculus is indirect-tax led: zero-rating, duty-free procurement and process discipline, with scheme-dependent legacy benefits belonging to older units. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between SEZ and STPI?

SEZ status is a geography-based regime under the SEZ Act 2005, offering direct and indirect-tax benefits within a demarcated area. STPI is a registration route under the software technology parks framework for IT and ITeS exporters, focused on indirect-tax relief.

### Are supplies to SEZ units zero-rated under GST?

Yes. Supplies of goods or services to an SEZ unit or developer for authorised operations are zero-rated under Section 16 of the IGST Act. Suppliers bill under a bond or LUT and claim refund of unutilised input tax credit, or pay tax and claim refund.

### Who can claim the Section 10AA deduction?

SEZ units that commenced manufacture or provision of services on or before 31 March 2021, subject to unit notification, deduction-claim conditions and scheme continuity. The 15-year schedule is scheme-dependent, so verify current positions before relying on it.

### What export obligations apply to an SEZ unit?

Units must achieve a positive net foreign exchange earning position over the block period set at approval. Shortfalls can trigger penalties or exit levies. STPI units file annual performance reports evidencing export realisation against projections.

### Can an SEZ unit sell in the domestic market?

Yes, within prescribed limits, but domestic tariff area sales attract the duties and levies that would otherwise apply to imports or taxable supplies. The unit pays those levies, and GST on DTA supplies follows normal rules with the recipient claiming credit.

### Is STPI registration still available to IT exporters?

Yes. IT and ITeS exporters can register under the software technology parks framework and access indirect-tax relief on qualifying exports, subject to registration conditions, statutory reporting and annual performance documentation.
