> LLP registration in India is the process of incorporating a Limited Liability Partnership under the LLP Act 2008 through the registry's RUN-LLP and FiLLiP filings, creating a body corporate where partners' liability is limited to their agreed contribution and the firm's existence is separate from its members.

An LLP suits professional firms, family businesses, joint ventures and service practices that want limited liability without company-grade compliance. {brand} handles LLP registration in India end to end — structure advice, filings, the LLP agreement, and the recurring compliance calendar that keeps the entity active and penalty-free.

## LLP vs Private Limited Company

Founders routinely ask us to choose between the two vehicles. The differences that decide the answer:

| Factor | LLP | Private Limited |
|---|---|---|
| Governing law | LLP Act 2008 | Companies Act 2013 |
| Annual filings | Form 8 and Form 11 | AOC-4, MGT-7, board processes |
| Audit | Only above Rs 40 lakh turnover or Rs 25 lakh contribution | Statutory audit mandatory for all |
| Equity funding and ESOPs | Not available; FDI on approval route | Shares and ESOPs; FDI largely automatic |
| Taxation | Flat 30% plus surcharge and cess; partners' share exempt u/s 10(2A) | 22% or 30% regime; dividends taxed in shareholders' hands |

If you plan to raise venture capital or grant ESOPs, a private limited company remains the right call — see our [company registration hub](/company-registration) for that route. Where the partners value contractual freedom and lighter filings, the LLP wins.

## How LLP Registration in India Works

1. **Digital Signature Certificates** — every proposed designated partner needs a DSC for online filing.
2. **Name reservation via RUN-LLP** — two name options are checked against existing entities and trademarks.
3. **Incorporation through FiLLiP** — the integrated form carries partner and witness details, registered office and the incorporation document (Form 2), with DIN allotment to designated partners and PAN/TAN applied alongside.
4. **LLP agreement in Form 3** — filed within thirty days of incorporation; this is the constitution of the firm, covering capital, profit-sharing, rights and exit terms.
5. **LLPIN and post-incorporation registrations** — GST where applicable, and Udyam for MSME benefits via our [MSME and Udyam registration](/msme-udyam-registration) service.

Documents are straightforward: PAN and Aadhaar of partners, address proof, photographs, registered office utility bill and owner NOC. Foreign partners provide apostilled passports and address proof.

## Annual Compliance After Incorporation

| Filing | Purpose | Due date |
|---|---|---|
| Form 3 | LLP agreement or amendments | Within 30 days of incorporation or change |
| Form 4 | Changes in partners or designated partners | Within 30 days of the change |
| Form 8 | Statement of Account and Solvency | 30 October |
| Form 11 | Annual Return | 30 May |
| DIR-3 KYC | KYC of designated partners holding DIN | Per the annual due date notified by the {corporate_affairs_authority} |

Statutory audit becomes mandatory once turnover crosses Rs 40 lakh or contribution crosses Rs 25 lakh. Delay attracts continuing penalty per day on Form 8 and Form 11 — a silent liability we clear for clients through our [ROC compliance](/roc-compliance) practice.

## Taxation Points LLPs Overlook

LLPs pay income tax at a flat 30% plus surcharge and cess. Partners' profit share is exempt in their hands u/s 10(2A), but remuneration and interest to partners must stay within the prescribed limits to remain deductible. From FY 2025-26, TDS u/s 194T applies to payments such as salary, remuneration, commission and interest to partners above the notified threshold. If an [audit](/tax-audit) applies, books and filings must meet the same discipline as a company's.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does LLP registration take?

Most LLPs are incorporated within one to three weeks of document readiness, subject to name approval and registry processing. The LLP agreement in Form 3 follows within thirty days of incorporation, completing the setup.

### Is audit mandatory for every LLP?

No. An LLP requires a statutory audit only if its turnover exceeds forty lakh rupees or its partners' contribution exceeds twenty-five lakh rupees in any financial year. Below these limits, only Form 8 and Form 11 filings apply.

### What are the annual compliance requirements for an LLP?

File Form 8, the Statement of Account and Solvency, by 30 October and Form 11, the Annual Return, by 30 May each year. DIR-3 KYC applies to designated partners, and Form 4 reports changes in partners.

### Can NRIs or foreign nationals be LLP partners?

Yes, but FDI in LLPs is permitted only under the approval route and operates on a downstream-investment basis. At least one resident designated partner is mandatory. Foreign-controlled LLPs need careful FEMA documentation.

### Can an LLP be converted into a private limited company?

Yes. An LLP can convert under Chapter X of the LLP Act 2008, subject to turnover and capital conditions and creditor consent. Many funded startups convert before raising equity, since LLPs cannot issue ESOPs or shares.

### Which forms report changes inside an LLP?

Form 3 records LLP agreement amendments, Form 4 partner and designated partner changes, Form 5 a name change, Form 15 a registered office shift and Form 12 where a second address is kept for service of documents. Orders of a court or tribunal affecting the LLP are intimated in Form 22.

### What if an LLP filing is defective or an offence needs compounding?

Filings marked defective or incomplete are corrected through a Form 32 addendum within the time the Registrar allows. Offences under the LLP Act can be compounded by applying in Form 31, and penalty orders are contested or settled through the LLP-ADJ adjudication mechanism. We assess each default and pick the route that minimises cost and exposure.

### Can a foreign LLP or foreign company establish a place of business in India?

Yes. A foreign LLP or foreign company reserves its name in Form 25, registers its particulars with the Registrar in Form 27 within 30 days of establishing a place of business, and reports later alterations or closure in Form 28. Beneficial ownership disclosures continue through Form 4D and Form BEN-2 where applicable.
