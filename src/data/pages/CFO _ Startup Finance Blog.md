## Burn Rate and Runway Discipline

Net burn is simply operating cash outflow minus inflows in a month; runway is cash on hand divided by that burn. The discipline lies in three habits: a rolling 12-month cash forecast updated monthly, a scenario buffer that shows survivable burn at a lower funding multiple, and a monthly close fast enough that the numbers are current when decisions are made. Boards increasingly probe the quality of spend — whether burn converts into durable revenue — rather than the raw figure. Founders who present burn alongside a milestone map, a hiring plan and a committed revenue pipeline negotiate from strength; those who present only a cash-out date invite dilution.

## ESOP Pool Planning Before You Scale Hiring

ESOP pool planning starts with the hiring plan, not the cap table. Indian startups typically reserve 10 to 15 percent of fully diluted equity, then work backwards: which senior hires, at what grant sizes, vesting over what schedule. Mechanically, a pool is created or topped up by shareholder resolution, usually through a fresh issue to an ESOP trust or a pre-round carve-out — both of which dilute existing holders and belong in the term sheet conversation. On tax, an option exercised is a salary perquisite measured at fair market value on the exercise date, subject to TDS under section 192; eligible startup employees can defer that collection until the earliest of 48 months from leaving, a sale of the shares, or five years from allotment. Scheme documents, valuation reports and grant registers should be audit-ready from the first grant.

## Angel Tax: The Legacy and the Current Regime

The angel tax under section 56(2)(viib) — the levy on share premiums received above fair market value — was abolished for all investors by the Finance Act 2025, effective from FY 2025-26, closing a decade-long friction point for early-stage capital. What survives is paperwork discipline: legacy years where the provision applied may still surface in assessments, so historical valuation reports and subscription documentation should be preserved. Separately, issuances at a premium continue to require defensible valuations under FEMA pricing guidelines for foreign investors, and section 68-style scrutiny of unexplained credits makes clean source-of-funds trails a permanent hygiene item rather than a tax-era relic. Our [startup tax exemptions guide](/startup-tax-exemptions) covers the reliefs that remain, including the section 80-IAC holiday for recognised startups, subject to its incorporation-window and recognition conditions.

## Unit Economics: The CFO-Grade Scorecard

Unit economics for startups reduce the business to one repeatable decision: does a unit of demand return more contribution than it costs? The working set is contribution margin per unit, CAC by channel, CAC payback period and retention by cohort. Forecast models built around these inputs — the kind we prepare for [fundraising and valuation](/financial-model-pitch-deck-valuation) — survive diligence because every line traces to an operational lever. The common failure is top-line CAC averaged across channels; the fix is channel-level cohorts with payback periods a CFO can defend.

## Funding-Stage Compliance Checklists

Compliance intensity rises with each round, and investors price diligence friction. A stage-wise baseline:

| Stage | Key finance and compliance checkpoints |
|---|---|
| Incorporation / pre-seed | Entity choice and constitution, founders' agreements, DPIIT recognition, clean cap table from day one |
| Angel / seed | Share valuation reports, CCPS or SAFE documentation, ESOP pool creation, preservation of section 56(2)(viib)-era records |
| Foreign capital | FEMA pricing compliance, FC-GPR and FC-TRS filings, sectoral cap review |
| Series A and beyond | Audit-ready books and MIS, ESOP scheme administration with payroll TDS controls, tax audit readiness |
| Growth horizon | Ind AS transition assessment, internal financial controls, secretarial hygiene for institutional rounds |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a healthy burn rate for a startup?

There is no universal number. A workable rule is a runway of at least 12 to 18 months at the current net burn, with burn growth slower than revenue growth. Discipline matters more than the absolute figure.

### How are ESOPs taxed in India?

ESOPs are taxed as salary perquisite at exercise on the spread between fair market value and exercise price, subject to TDS under section 192. Gains on later sale are capital gains. Eligible startup employees may defer the perquisite tax.

### Is angel tax still applicable in India?

No. The angel tax under section 56(2)(viib) was abolished for all investors by the Finance Act 2025, effective from FY 2025-26. Legacy assessments for earlier years may still require handling, and valuation documentation remains prudent.

### How large should an ESOP pool be?

Indian startups commonly reserve 10 to 15 percent of fully diluted equity, calibrated to hiring plans, expected senior hires and the dilution negotiated with investors in each round.

### What should be ready before a funding round?

Clean books with a monthly close, a defensible valuation report, reconciled statutory dues, cap table accuracy and executed investment documents under FEMA pricing guidelines where capital is foreign.

### Why does an early startup need a virtual CFO?

A virtual CFO installs investor-grade reporting, cash and runway control, ESOP and compliance calendars before a full-time finance team is viable, at a fraction of the cost of an in-house hire.
