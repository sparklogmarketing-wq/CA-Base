## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the due date for filing income tax returns?

July 31 for individuals and non-audit cases; October 31 where a tax audit applies. Belated returns can generally be filed by December 31 of the assessment year with late fees under section 234F.

### Can I file an updated return if I missed income?

Yes. Section 139(8A) permits an updated return (ITR-U) within 24 months of the end of the relevant assessment year, with additional tax on the updated liability. We assess exposure before filing.

### When is advance tax payable, and how much?

In four instalments: 15% by June 15, 45% by September 15, 75% by December 15 and 100% by March 15. Shortfalls attract interest under sections 234B and 234C.

### What are Form 26AS, AIS and TIS?

They are pre-filled tax data statements: Form 26AS shows TDS and taxes paid, while the Annual Information Statement and Taxpayer Information Summary report your reported financial transactions. We reconcile all three before filing to avoid mismatches.

### Which ITR form applies to my business?

It depends on constitution and income type: ITR-3 for proprietors with business income, ITR-5 for firms and LLPs, and ITR-6 for companies. We confirm the correct form after reviewing your income streams.
