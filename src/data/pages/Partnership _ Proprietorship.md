> Partnership firm and proprietorship registration covers the two simplest business formats in India: a proprietorship recognised through the owner's registrations (Udyam, GST, shops & establishment), and a partnership firm formed under the Indian Partnership Act 1932 with a deed registered before the Registrar of Firms. ## Proprietorship vs Partnership vs LLP

| Factor | Proprietorship | Partnership Firm | LLP |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owners | One | Two or more partners | Two or more partners |
| Governing framework | No dedicated statute | Indian Partnership Act 1932 | LLP Act 2008 |
| Liability | Unlimited | Unlimited | Limited to contribution |
| Taxation | Slab rates on the individual | 30% flat plus surcharge and cess | 30% flat plus surcharge and cess |
| Formal registration | Via Udyam, GST, shops & establishment | Deed + Registrar of Firms | FiLLiP + Form 3 |

A proprietorship fits solo operators testing an idea; a registered partnership suits family firms and trading businesses with shared capital; the moment liability or outside investment matters, upgrade to an LLP or a [company](/company-registration).

## Sole Proprietorship Registration: What It Actually Involves

There is no single proprietorship certificate — sole proprietorship registration is established through a stack of documents that banks and clients accept:

- **Udyam (MSME) registration** in the proprietor's name — see our [MSME and Udyam registration](/msme-udyam-registration) service for the benefits it unlocks
- **GST registration** where turnover crosses the prescribed threshold or inter-state supplies, e-commerce selling or reverse-charge cases apply
- **Shops and establishment registration** under the {state} Shops and Commercial Establishments Act for the business premises
- **Current account** opened against these registrations, plus trade licences where the line of business requires them

The owner's PAN serves as the business PAN, and income is taxed at individual slab rates — with Section 44AD offering the simplification described below.

## Partnership Firm Registration Process

1. **Draft the partnership deed** — capital contributions, profit-sharing ratio, roles, admission and retirement clauses, and dispute resolution, executed on stamp paper of the value prescribed in {state}.
2. **PAN and TAN for the firm** — applied in the firm's name.
3. **Registration with the Registrar of Firms** — filing the deed and prescribed forms so the firm can enforce contractual rights against third parties.
4. **Operational registrations** — GST where applicable, Udyam for MSME benefits, shops & establishment for premises, and a current account in the firm's name.

Registration is technically optional under the 1932 Act, but an unregistered firm cannot sue to enforce its contractual rights — we register by default.

## The Section 44AD Presumptive Taxation Interplay

Presumptive taxation is where these structures earn their keep:

- **Section 44AD** lets eligible businesses declare 8% of cash turnover or 6% of digital receipts as deemed profit, without maintaining detailed books. The turnover ceiling is Rs 2 crore, rising to Rs 3 crore where at least 95% of receipts and payments are digital.
- **Section 44ADA** extends presumptive logic to specified professionals at 50% of gross receipts, up to Rs 75 lakh (Rs 50 lakh in the default case).
- Declaring profits below presumptive rates — or crossing the limits — pulls the business into books and a potential audit u/s 44AB, where our [tax audit](/tax-audit) team steps in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is registration compulsory for a partnership firm?

Registration under the Indian Partnership Act 1932 is not mandatory, but an unregistered firm cannot sue third parties to enforce contract rights. Most firms register with the Registrar of Firms to preserve remedies and satisfy bank requirements.

### Does a proprietorship have a separate PAN?

No. A proprietorship uses the owner's PAN, and business income is taxed as the individual's income at slab rates. Identity for the business is established through Udyam, GST and shops & establishment registrations.

### How does Section 44AD reduce compliance?

Eligible businesses can declare 8% of cash receipts or 6% of digital receipts as presumptive profit, up to the prescribed turnover ceiling, with no books-based audit. The limit rises where at least 95% of receipts and payments are digital.

### Can a proprietorship have two owners?

No. A proprietorship has a single owner by definition. Two or more persons need a partnership firm, LLP or company. Converting later is straightforward with proper deed or incorporation drafting.

### Is shops and establishment registration required in Karnataka?

Yes, most commercial establishments in {state} must register under the Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, typically within the prescribed period of commencing business. We handle the filing alongside Udyam and GST.
