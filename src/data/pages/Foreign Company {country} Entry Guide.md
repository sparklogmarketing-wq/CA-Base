> Foreign company registration in {country} is the process of establishing a legally recognised presence — a wholly-owned subsidiary, joint venture, branch office or liaison office — under the Companies Act 2013 and FEMA 1999. The right choice balances ownership, tax exposure, repatriation and the speed at which the business can trade.

Overseas groups rarely get a second chance at first compliance. The entry structure fixed on day one determines approvals, tax rates, hiring freedom and eventual exit. This guide walks the decision tree {brand} uses with inbound clients, then sets out realistic timelines and an onboarding checklist that keeps the new {country} operation clean from its first rupee.

## The Entry Decision Tree: Liaison → Branch → Subsidiary → JV

Think of entry as a ladder, not a single jump:

1. **Sales liaison office** — a low-cost, revenue-free market study. It can represent the parent and promote exports but cannot invoice a single customer. Suited to genuine market validation.
2. **Branch office** — for established parents meeting RBI track-record tests, a branch executes limited activities such as export-import support, consultancy or technical services, taxed at foreign-company rates.
3. **Wholly-owned subsidiary** — the default for real operations: full commercial freedom, domestic-company taxation and unrestricted hiring. See our [subsidiary incorporation](/subsidiary-entity) service for the process.
4. **Joint venture** — where sector rules, sourcing capability or distribution assets demand a local partner, a JV company splits ownership under the [FDI rules](/fdi-in-company-llp).

Most groups that begin with a liaison or branch office eventually incorporate; planning the migration early avoids restructuring costs later.

## Entry Modes Compared

| Mode | Approval | Revenue | Parent eligibility | Indicative use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Liaison office | RBI via AD Category-I bank | None | 3 profit years; net worth USD 50,000+ | Market study, brand presence |
| Branch office | RBI via AD Category-I bank | Permitted activities only | 5 profit years; net worth USD 100,000+ | Limited trading, research, support |
| Wholly-owned subsidiary | Automatic route (most sectors) | Full | None beyond FDI policy | Full operations |
| Joint venture | Automatic or approval, per sector | Full | Per sectoral rules | Partner-dependent strategies |

## Realistic Timelines

| Step | Indicative duration |
|---|---|
| Structuring, sector check and documentation | 1–2 weeks |
| Attestation or apostille of parent documents | Varies with the parent's jurisdiction |
| Name reservation and SPICe+ incorporation | Typically 2–4 weeks |
| PAN, TAN, bank account, GST registration | Concurrent to a few weeks after incorporation |
| Branch or liaison office approval | AD bank and RBI processing; no fixed statutory timeline |

Treat every figure as indicative: registry workload, name objections and document legalisation vary case by case. What is controllable — document quality and correct filings — is where we focus.

## Compliance-First Onboarding Checklist

- Confirm the sector's FDI position, including Press Note 3 exposure, before money moves.
- Fix the capital structure and valuation approach consistent with FDI pricing guidelines.
- Secure a registered office and identify a resident director under Section 149(3).
- Report the inward remittance and file Form FC-GPR within 30 days of allotment.
- Register for PAN, TAN and GST at the right moment; open the {country} bank account promptly.
- Appoint the statutory auditor within 30 days and stand up bookkeeping, payroll and TDS processes.
- Set the annual calendar: FLA return by July 15, ROC filings, income tax and, where relevant, transfer pricing documentation — our [FEMA and RBI reporting](/fema-RBI-reporting) team maintains this cycle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best entry mode for a foreign company in {country}?

Most groups graduate through the ladder: a liaison office to study the market, a branch office for limited activities, then a wholly-owned subsidiary for full operations. Joint ventures suit sectors or strategies that need a local partner. The subsidiary is the endpoint for almost every serious commercial commitment.

### How long does foreign company registration in {country} take?

A wholly-owned subsidiary typically incorporates within two to four weeks once the parent's documents are attested and the name is cleared, though processing varies. Branch and liaison office approvals depend on AD bank and Reserve Bank processing, and bank account activation adds further days.

### Can a foreign company sell in {country} without registering an entity?

Limited routes exist — appointing a distributor or using a marketplace platform keeps revenue outside the group — but a liaison office cannot earn revenue and a branch office cannot do retail trading. Sustained direct selling normally requires a subsidiary.

### Is 100% foreign ownership allowed in {country}?

In most sectors, yes — FDI up to 100% is permitted under the automatic route. A minority of sectors carry caps or approval requirements, and investors from countries sharing a land border with {country} need Government approval under Press Note 3.

### What are the first compliance filings after entry?

After incorporation, report the inward remittance and file Form FC-GPR within 30 days of allotting shares, register for GST where taxable supplies begin, appoint a statutory auditor within 30 days, and file the annual FLA return by July 15 alongside regular income tax and ROC filings.
