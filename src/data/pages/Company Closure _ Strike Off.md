> A private limited company strike off is a registrar-led exit under Section 248 of the Companies Act, 2013, by which the Registrar of Companies removes the name of a defunct or non-operational company from the register, dissolving the entity without the cost and duration of winding up proceedings.

## What Is a Company Strike Off Under Section 248?

Section 248 of the Companies Act, 2013 allows the Registrar of Companies (RoC) under the {corporate_affairs_authority} to remove a company's name in two ways: suo motu for defunct companies, or on the company's own application — the fast track exit. The application is made in Form STK-2 with the prescribed fee. Once satisfied, the RoC publishes the name for objections, and on removal the company stands dissolved from the date of the order; the certificate of incorporation is deemed cancelled.

Two safeguards matter. Under Section 248(3), dissolution does not extinguish the liability of officers in default or of the company for pre-dissolution acts. And an aggrieved party can seek restoration under Section 252 before the {company_law_tribunal} within twenty years of dissolution — which is why closure should still be done properly.

## Strike Off vs Winding Up: Choosing the Right Exit

| Aspect | Strike off (Section 248, STK-2 filing) | Winding up |
|---|---|---|
| Forum | Registrar of Companies | {company_law_tribunal} for compulsory winding up; voluntary liquidation under Section 59 of the IBC, 2016 |
| Best suited to | Defunct, debt-free companies with nil assets and liabilities | Companies with assets, creditors, borrowings or disputes to settle |
| Professional involvement | Directors, auditors and the RoC | Liquidator, registered valuers and, in supervised cases, the tribunal |
| Duration and cost | Comparatively short and low cost | Materially longer and more expensive |
| Asset distribution | Assets distributed to members or surrendered before applying | Assets realised and distributed by the liquidator under statutory waterfall |

Where the company carries secured borrowings, litigation, tax dues or assets needing distribution among members, strike off is rejected or simply the wrong route — winding up or voluntary liquidation is the correct company closure in India.

## Eligibility Conditions for STK-2 Filing

A company qualifies for the fast track exit where any one of these conditions holds: it has not carried on business or operations for the two immediately preceding financial years and has made no significant transaction in that period; it has not filed financial statements and annual returns for those two years; or it was incorporated within the preceding year and has never commenced business. Section 8 companies may also apply after obtaining prior approval from the regional director.

The route is blocked where the company has pending charges without secured-creditor no-objection, is listed or delisted without final orders, faces an inspection, inquiry or prosecution, has outstanding dues to banks, financial institutions or debenture holders, unpaid taxes, or a rejected application under challenge.

## Director Disqualification and Other Consequences

Directors feel the cost of an abandoned company for years. Where a company fails to file financial statements or annual returns for five consecutive financial years, every director is disqualified under Section 164(2) for five years and must vacate office in every other company under Section 167. Separately, DINs are deactivated for non-filing of DIR-3 KYC, freezing filings in the director's name — our [director KYC and SBO services](/directors-kyc-sbo) handle such reactivations. A strike off cures none of these defaults; regularising overdue [ROC compliance filings](/roc-compliance) before applying is what protects the directors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a company with overdue ROC filings be struck off?

Not directly. Pending financial statements and annual returns must first be filed with additional fees; once current, STK-2 can be filed. We regularise the backlog and then complete the strike off.

### What is the difference between strike off and winding up?

Strike off under Section 248 is a registrar-led removal of the name for defunct, debt-free companies, done through Form STK-2 without tribunal intervention. Winding up involves a liquidator realising assets and settling creditors, often under tribunal supervision.

### What happens to directors after a company is struck off?

The company ceases to exist, but personal liabilities survive under Section 248(3). If the company failed to file annual returns for five consecutive years, directors face five-year disqualification under Section 164(2) and possible DIN deactivation.

### How long does the STK-2 strike off process take?

Typically four to six months from filing, depending on RoC scrutiny and the public objection window. Backlog filings, pending charges or uncancelled registrations extend the timeline.

### Is an LLP closed the same way?

No. LLPs are struck off under Form 24 of the LLP Rules, a similar fast track route requiring partner consent, up-to-date statements of account and solvency, and clearance of any outstanding filings.

### Is strike off the only way to close an inactive company?

No. Where the company may be needed again — holding land, licences or a brand — dormant company status is the lighter alternative: MSC-1 obtains the status, MSC-3 is the annual return, and MSC-4 restores active status. Strike off through STK-2 remains the permanent exit when the entity will never operate again.
