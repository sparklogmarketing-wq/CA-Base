> GST rates in India are notified by the {indirect_tax_authority} under the CGST and IGST Acts. Since the restructure effective 22 September 2025, the structure centres on two main slabs of 5% and 18%, with a 40% rate for demerit goods, alongside exemptions and special rates for select categories.

## What Are the Current GST Rates in India?

GST rates in India are set through rate notifications issued by the {indirect_tax_authority} following decisions of the GST Council. After the restructure that took effect on 22 September 2025, the rate architecture is far simpler than the pre-2025 multi-slab system: the 12% and 28% slabs were dismantled, most items were redistributed into 5% or 18%, and a single 40% rate replaced the old combination of a 28% slab with compensation cess for demerit products.

| Category | Current treatment | Indicative examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exempt and nil-rated | No GST, or 0% | Fresh produce, unbranded staples; individual life and health insurance premiums |
| Merit rate | 5% | Most daily-use and common consumer items moved down from 12% |
| Standard rate | 18% | Most goods and services remaining after reclassification |
| Demerit rate | 40% | Pan masala, aerated sugary drinks, luxury cars, yachts, private aircraft; betting, casinos and similar services |
| Special rates | Continues at specific rates | Gold, silver and jewellery at 3%; concessional rates on certain precious stones; zero-rating for exports |

The list above is indicative, not exhaustive. Classification is always HSN-wise, and borderline items — composite supplies, food preparations, accessories and parts — are where rate disputes arise.

## The September 2025 Restructure in Plain Terms

The restructure, implemented on the recommendations of the GST Council with effect from 22 September 2025, was designed to cut tax on common consumption while taxing sin and luxury goods harder. In practical terms:

- Essentials and merit goods migrated to 5% or exemptions, reducing input costs for households and for businesses downstream.
- Most services, electronics and other goods previously at 28% moved to 18%, improving credit-neutral pricing across business-to-business chains.
- Demerit goods and services consolidated at the 40% rate; tobacco products continue under the earlier compensation-cess arrangement pending the notified transition to the new rate.
- Accommodation below the notified nightly value and restaurant services saw rate rationalisation without input tax credit, which matters for hospitality costing.

For exporters the principle is unchanged: outward supplies under export are zero-rated, as detailed in our guide on [GST for exports and zero-rated supplies](/gst-exports).

## Classification: HSN Codes and Notifications

Every rate question starts with the HSN code for goods or SAC code for services. Turnover-linked HSN disclosure rules apply on invoices and in [GST return filing](/gst-return-filing), so a wrong code creates two problems at once — a wrong rate now and an incorrect HSN trail in later audits. When a notification is ambiguous, the {indirect_tax_authority} issues circulars clarifying classification, and advance rulings under the CGST Act can bind the applicant for its specific facts. ## Managing Rate Changes in Your Business

A rate change is a working-capital event. Cut-over discipline includes applying the new rate to supplies made on or after the effective date, treating advances received earlier correctly, issuing credit notes where prices are revised, and reconciling GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B for the transition month. Where a restructure reduces rates, review contracts with price-variation clauses and pass on the benefit; where rates rise, check input-side credit availability to protect margins on both ends.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the current GST slabs after the September 2025 restructure?

Since 22 September 2025, most goods and services fall under two main slabs of 5% and 18%, with a 40% rate reserved for demerit products such as pan masala, aerated drinks and luxury vehicles. The earlier 12% and 28% slabs were merged away.

### Which items moved to 5% under the restructure?

Daily-use and common items previously in the 12% band were largely moved to 5%, and individual life and health insurance premiums became exempt. Classifications are category-specific, so HSN-wise confirmation against current notifications is essential before pricing.

### What is the 40% demerit rate?

It is a single high rate that replaced the old 28% slab plus compensation cess for goods such as pan masala, aerated beverages, luxury cars, yachts and private aircraft, and for betting, casinos and similar services, with tobacco products transitioning under the earlier cess arrangement.

### How do I find the GST rate for my product?

Identify the HSN code for goods or the SAC code for services, then check the current rate notification and applicable clarifications issued by the {indirect_tax_authority}. Where interpretation is disputed, advance rulings provide binding certainty for your facts.

### Did gold and jewellery rates change?

Gold, silver and jewellery continue at 3%, separate from the main slab structure, while rough diamonds retain their concessional treatment. Watch notifications for any movement in the precious-metal category.

### How should businesses handle a rate change?

Revise price lists, update ERP masters and invoice formats, apply the correct rate to supplies on or after the effective date, adjust advances and review credit notes, then reconcile GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B for the transition month.
