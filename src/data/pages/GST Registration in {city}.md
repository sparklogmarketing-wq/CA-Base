> GST registration in {city} is the mandatory enrolment of a business under the CGST Act, 2017 and the Karnataka State GST Act once aggregate turnover crosses Rs 40 lakh for goods or Rs 20 lakh for services. It results in a 15-digit GSTIN, enabling lawful tax collection and input tax credit.

## Who Needs GST Registration in {city}?

{city} is {state}'s commercial engine — IT parks, D2C brands, traders and one of {country}'s largest startup ecosystems. The same statutory tests apply to every one of them:

| Category | Threshold / trigger in {city} |
| --- | --- |
| Suppliers of goods only | Rs 40 lakh aggregate turnover |
| Service providers | Rs 20 lakh aggregate turnover |
| Inter-state suppliers of goods | Mandatory, no threshold |
| Sellers on e-commerce platforms | Mandatory (Section 24, CGST Act) |
| Casual taxable persons, agents | Mandatory, no threshold |

Karnataka is not a special-category state, so the reduced Rs 10 lakh limit for hill and north-eastern states does not apply here. Even below threshold, voluntary online GST registration often makes commercial sense: input tax credit on rent, laptops and inventory, and credibility with corporate customers who insist on GST invoices.

## Documents Required for GST Registration

The documents required for GST registration differ by constitution, but the core set is stable:

| Document | What is accepted |
| --- | --- |
| PAN | PAN of the business or proprietor; company/LLP PAN for corporates |
| Identity proof | Aadhaar and PAN of promoters, partners or directors |
| Principal place of business | Electricity bill or property tax receipt, plus owned/rent agreement or owner consent letter |
| Bank proof | Cancelled cheque or first page of bank statement |
| Photographs | Passport-size photos of promoters |
| Digital signature | Required for companies and LLPs; authorised signatory letter where applicable |

Address mismatches are the largest cause of rejection in {city}. If you operate from a co-working space or rented premises, secure the owner's consent letter and a utility bill in the owner's name before filing — not after a notice arrives.

## GST Registration Process Online: Step by Step

The GST registration process in {city} is fully digital through the {indirect_tax_authority} portal:

1. **PART-A:** Submit PAN, mobile, email and state; receive a TRN.
2. **PART-B:** Complete FORM GST REG-01 with business details, promoter KYC, HSN codes and address proofs.
3. **Aadhaar authentication:** Promoters authorise and verify Aadhaar OTP — this avoids physical site verification.
4. **ARN generation:** An Application Reference Number is issued on submission. No government fee is payable.
5. **Officer review:** Where Aadhaar is authenticated, approval in FORM GST REG-06 typically issues within seven working days. Queries arrive on FORM GST REG-03, with seven days to reply.
6. **GSTIN allotted:** You receive a 15-digit GSTIN, enabling e-way bill generation and GST invoicing immediately.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who needs GST registration in {city}?

Any business in {city} whose aggregate turnover exceeds Rs 40 lakh for goods or Rs 20 lakh for services must register. Registration is also mandatory for inter-state goods suppliers, e-commerce sellers, casual taxable persons and reverse-charge recipients, irrespective of turnover.

### How long does GST registration take?

Where Aadhaar is authenticated online and documents are in order, approval typically comes within seven working days of the ARN. If Aadhaar authentication is skipped or the officer raises a query on FORM GST REG-03, approval can take up to about three weeks.

### What is the government fee for GST registration?

The government charges no fee for GST registration. You only bear professional fees if you engage a consultant. Beware of agents selling addresses; the principal place of business must be genuine and supported by ownership proof, a rent agreement or a consent letter.

### Can I register under GST below the threshold?

Yes. Voluntary registration is allowed and often sensible for {city} startups selling to registered businesses or on marketplaces, since input tax credit on purchases becomes available and many B2B customers insist on dealing with GST-registered vendors.

### What are the penalties for not registering?

Operating without mandatory registration attracts tax, interest and a penalty of 10% of the tax due or Rs 10,000, whichever is higher. Where evasion is deliberate with intent to evade, the penalty can rise to 100% of the tax under Section 122 of the CGST Act, 2017.

### Do I need a separate GSTIN for each state?

Yes. GST registration is state-wise. A {city} business needs one GSTIN for Karnataka; if it opens a warehouse or branch in another state, a separate registration is required there, even within the same legal entity.
