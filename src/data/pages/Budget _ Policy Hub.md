## Union Budget Coverage Each Year

The Union Budget sets the direct tax agenda: slab and rate changes, deduction and TDS amendments, compliance reliefs and startup or capital-market measures proposed by the {direct_tax_authority}. Two disciplines matter when reading Budget coverage. First, a proposal is not law — it takes effect only when the Finance Act is enacted, and often from a specified date. Second, the indirect tax announcements relating to the {indirect_tax_authority} frequently take effect on different dates from the direct tax provisions, so implementation planning should follow the actual notification rather than the speech.

## Policy Changes Currently Reshaping Compliance

Several structural changes define the current compliance environment:

- **GST rate rationalisation:** from September 2025, GST runs on two main slabs — 5 per cent and 18 per cent — plus a 40 per cent demerit rate. Businesses re-mapped HSN and SAC classifications, revised price lists and checked MRP-labelled stock during the transition.
- **Updated returns:** ITR-U under section 139(8A) permits correcting a missed or defective filing within 24 months of the end of the relevant assessment year, on payment of additional tax.
- **Angel tax abolition:** the deemed-dividend charge on premium share issues — section 56(2)(viib) — stands abolished for all investors from FY 2025-26 by the Finance Act 2025.
- **Dispute settlement:** the Vivad se Vishwas scheme has concluded, so legacy direct-tax disputes move through the normal appellate chain, including faceless proceedings.
- **Faceless assessment:** scrutiny under section 144 continues to run through the national faceless framework, which rewards well-documented replies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When do Budget tax announcements take effect?
Most direct tax changes apply from April 1 of the following financial year; indirect tax changes apply on notified dates. Proposals bind taxpayers only after the Finance Act is enacted.

### Did GST slabs change recently?
Yes. From September 2025, the GST structure runs on two main slabs of 5 per cent and 18 per cent, with a 40 per cent rate for demerit goods and services.

### Where can I check current GST rates?
Classify the product by HSN or SAC and verify on the {indirect_tax_authority} portal. Our GST rates and notifications page summarises major revisions in business terms.

### What is ITR-U and how far back does it reach?
ITR-U is an updated return under section 139(8A) for missed or incorrect filings, available within 24 months from the end of the relevant assessment year, with additional tax.

### Is the Vivad se Vishwas scheme still open?
No. The direct-tax dispute settlement scheme has concluded, so unresolved matters now proceed through ordinary appellate routes; any fresh window would require new legislation.
