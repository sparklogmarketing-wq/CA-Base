## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should a business engage a tax consultant?

Ideally from inception — structure, registrations and early filings shape your tax position for years. At minimum, engage before crossing GST thresholds, hiring staff, raising capital or receiving any notice from tax authorities.

### Can a tax consultant represent me before the income tax department?

Yes. Chartered accountants are authorised representatives under the Income-tax Act and can appear, file submissions and argue assessments and appeals on your behalf, including faceless assessment proceedings before the national faceless assessment centre.

### Do you handle GST along with income tax?

Yes. Most engagements cover both: GST registration, returns, ITC reconciliation and notices, alongside income tax filings, TDS, advance tax and planning. Coordinated handling keeps your GST and income tax positions consistent.

### How do you charge for tax advisory work?

Fees are quoted per engagement or as an annual retainer based on the scope — number of filings, notice exposure and advisory intensity. We share a written quote after a scoping discussion; there are no hidden charges.

### Can you help me choose between the old and new tax regimes?
