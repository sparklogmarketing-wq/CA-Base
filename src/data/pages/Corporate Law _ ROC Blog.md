## SPICe+ and the Evolution of Incorporation

Incorporation has evolved into SPICe+: an integrated web submission combining name reservation, incorporation, PAN and TAN allotment, EPFO and ESI registration and bank account opening. Recent amendment cycles have tightened the form — fewer resubmissions, stricter auto-checks — so clean documentation matters more. ## The CSR Regime: Registration, Spend and Reporting

Section 135 has matured from a spend obligation into a reporting regime. Eligible companies must constitute a CSR committee, adopt a policy and report through prescribed forms; implementing agencies must register before receiving funds, and unspent amounts follow strict transfer routes. Our [CSR compliance](/csr-compliance) services cover eligibility, governance and annual filing.

## Significant Beneficial Ownership: The SBO Framework

The SBO framework under section 90 requires every company to look through its register and identify individuals who ultimately hold or control ten percent or more of shares, voting rights or dividend rights, reported through BEN-1 and BEN-2 with returns such as MGT-6. Layered structures routinely hide an SBO in a shelf entity, and non-filing invites restrictions on the affected shares. Our [director KYC and SBO](/directors-kyc-sbo) practice handles the full declaration chain.

## Dematerialisation of Private Company Shares

Private companies other than small companies must operate in dematerialised form: further issues and transfers are accepted only against demat holdings, and the company must secure an ISIN through a registered depository participant. Founders, ESOP holders and incoming investors should dematerialise existing certificates, since physical holdings can stall a funding round or succession transfer. Our [dematerialisation of shares](/dematerialisation-shares) service manages the migration end to end.

## Penalty Rationalisation and Adjudication

Successive amendment cycles have moved many procedural defaults to in-house adjudication, scaled some penalties to turnover and capped penalties for continuing failures. Technical lapses now attract monetary discipline while fraud remains criminal — raising the cost of missed ROC filing updates, and strengthening the case for our [ROC compliance](/roc-compliance) retainers. Where a company is years behind, compounding or a clean [strike-off](/company-strike-off) is usually cheaper than accruing daily additional fees.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the annual ROC filings for a private company?

Core filings include AOC-4 for financial statements, MGT-7 for the annual return, DIR-3 KYC for every director, DPT-3 for deposits reporting and MSME Form 1 where applicable, within prescribed time. Additional fees accrue daily on delay.

### Who must declare significant beneficial ownership?

Any individual who directly or indirectly holds or controls ten percent or more of a company's shares, voting rights or dividend rights must report under section 90, with the company filing the declaration in BEN-2.

### Is dematerialisation of shares mandatory for private companies?

Yes. Under rule 9B of the Prospectus and Allotment of Securities Rules, private companies other than small companies must issue further shares and effect transfers only in dematerialised form, so shareholders should dematerialise holdings.

### Which companies need CSR registration and reporting?

Companies meeting any section 135 threshold in the preceding year — net worth of Rs 500 crore, turnover of Rs 1,000 crore or net profit of Rs 5 crore — must constitute a CSR committee, spend the prescribed amount and file annual reporting.

### What did penalty rationalisation change under the Companies Act?

Many technical defaults were reclassified for in-house adjudication instead of prosecution, and penalties for continuing defaults were restructured and capped. Procedural non-compliance now attracts monetary discipline rather than criminal litigation.

### How does SPICe+ simplify company incorporation?

SPICe+ is an integrated web form covering name reservation, incorporation, PAN and TAN allotment, EPFO, ESI and bank account opening in one submission, with AGILE-PRO adding GST and professional tax registrations.
