## Residency, Treaties and the Compliance Calendar

Most cross-border disputes begin with residency. Corporate residence follows place of effective management; individual residence follows the day-count tests in Section 6, with deemed residency for high-income Indian citizens. Once residence is fixed, the applicable DTAA allocates taxing rights and the foreign tax credit rules prevent residual double taxation — claimed in Form 67 with foreign certificates. Around that analysis sits a compliance calendar that cannot slip: Form 3CEB by 31 October, master file and CbCR under Section 286, Form 15CA/15CB before repatriation, and ITR disclosures that reconcile to Form 26AS and AIS.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is DTAA in simple terms?

A Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement is a treaty between two countries that decides which one can tax a given income and how relief is granted, so the same rupee of income is not fully taxed twice — once where it arises and once where the recipient resides.

### Do NRIs have to pay tax in India?

NRIs are taxed only on income received or accrued in India — NRO interest, rent, dividends and capital gains. NRE and FCNR interest is exempt while non-resident. Treaty rates can reduce withholding, and returns claim refunds of excess TDS.

### How is foreign tax credit claimed?

Tax paid abroad on income also taxable in India is credited under Section 90 or 91 read with the prescribed rules, claimed in Form 67, and supported by the foreign tax certificate. The credit cannot exceed Indian tax on that income.

### When do transfer pricing rules apply?

Whenever an Indian entity transacts with an associated enterprise — services, royalty, loans, guarantees or share issues. Arm's length pricing must be documented under Rule 10D and certified in Form 3CEB by 31 October of the assessment year.

### Which structure should a foreign company choose for India?

A private limited subsidiary suits commercial operations; branch or liaison offices suit narrower purposes with RBI approval. Each carries different FEMA reporting, tax rates and repatriation mechanics, so the choice depends on activity, funding and exit plans.
