> Startup funding and valuation services prepare a company to raise capital from angels and venture capital funds — choosing the right instrument for the stage, producing Rule 11UA-compliant valuations, negotiating term-sheet economics and completing the company law, FEMA and tax filings that follow every allotment. A funding round is one of the few events where tax law, company law, FEMA and negotiation all meet in the same week. Founders who treat it as only a legal exercise usually over-give; founders who treat it as only a negotiation usually create compliance debt that surfaces at the next round or the diligence before an [IPO](/ipo-sme-ipo). ## Funding Stages and Instruments: From Pre-Seed to Series C

| Stage | Typical investors | Common instruments |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-seed | Founders, friends and family, incubators, angel networks | SAFE-style compulsorily convertible notes (the Indian adaptation under the ASIIF framework), plain equity |
| Seed | Angel investors, syndicates, micro-VCs | SAFE-style notes, CCPS, compulsorily convertible debentures |
| Series A | Institutional venture funds | Series A CCPS with negotiated preference terms |
| Series B | Venture and growth funds | Series B CCPS, step-up valuations, tighter covenants |
| Series C and beyond | Late-stage funds, cross-over investors | CCPS, structured instruments ahead of listing |

Two structural notes matter for founders. First, CCPS — compulsorily convertible preference shares — has become the default priced-round instrument in India because it satisfies the foreign investment pricing guidelines while carrying negotiated preference rights. Second, deferred-valuation instruments like SAFE-style notes avoid a priced valuation early but still convert against a priced round later, so the Round One terms quietly set the Round Two cap table.

## The Angel Tax: What Changed and What Still Applies

The angel tax, introduced under Section 56(2)(viib) of the Income-tax Act 1961, taxed closely held companies on share premium received above the fair market value — first from resident investors, later extended to non-resident investors, and burdened startups with valuation disputes for over a decade. It stands abolished for all investors with effect from FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), removing the single most contentious line in early-stage funding.

Abolition does not end documentation. Share premium can still be examined as an unexplained credit under Section 68, and gift and general assessment principles still apply to allotments. Our practice now centres on a clean paper trail per round: a Rule 11UA valuation supporting the price, board and shareholder resolutions, private placement documentation, and source-of-funds records — so premium is defensible if any future assessment asks. Founders planning [DPIIT recognition](/startup-registration-dpiit) should also keep the exemption documentation, such as the [Section 80-IAC tax holiday](/startup-tax-exemptions) conditions, aligned with the cap table from day one.

## Reading a VC Term Sheet: The Economics That Decide Your Exit

Term sheets are mostly non-binding, but the economics they fix bind through the definitive agreements. The three clauses that move founder outcomes the most:

- **Liquidation preference** — investors recover a defined multiple of invested value before ordinary shareholders; watch the multiple and whether the preference participates after recovery.
- **Anti-dilution** — protection against down-round pricing, typically broad-based weighted average; full ratchet clauses deserve hard negotiation.
- **ESOP pool** — a top-up demanded before closing shrinks effective founder ownership; model it pre-money, not post-money.

Every term must be tested against the [financial model](/financial-model-pitch-deck-valuation) and a cap-table simulation across raise, flat and down scenarios before signature.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is angel tax still applicable in India?

No. The angel tax under Section 56(2)(viib) stands abolished for all investors with effect from FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27). Valuation documentation discipline continues regardless, because share premium can still be examined under Section 68 unexplained credits in assessments.

### What is a Rule 11UA valuation?

Rule 11UA of the Income-tax Rules prescribes how the fair market value of unquoted equity shares is computed, using the net asset value method or the discounted free cash flow method. A documented valuation supporting each round's price is the backbone of premium defensibility.

### Why do investors prefer CCPS in Indian startups?

Compulsorily convertible preference shares give investors downside protection and defined liquidation rights while remaining convertible into equity, and they satisfy the foreign investment pricing guidelines. Founders retain ordinary equity and voting control while the capital works.

### What is a liquidation preference in a term sheet?

It entitles the investor to receive a defined amount — commonly one times the invested value — from a sale or liquidation before ordinary shareholders participate. Non-participating versions let investors instead convert and share pro-rata when that pays more.

### What compliance follows a foreign VC investment?

The issue price must satisfy the FEMA pricing guidelines, shares are allotted with the board and shareholder approvals, and Form FC-GPR is filed with the RBI within the prescribed time of allotment, along with any sector-specific conditionality that applies.
