> FEMA and RBI reporting is the set of mandatory filings — FC-GPR, FC-TRS, Form DI, the annual FLA return and activity reports — through which foreign-invested {country} entities report foreign capital, share transfers and overseas investments to the Reserve Bank of India under FEMA 1999. Timely reporting keeps investment, dividend and exit routes open.

Foreign investment in {country} is permitted generously but policed strictly. The Reserve Bank of {country} does not approve every transaction; instead it relies on a web of time-bound reports filed through AD Category-I banks. Miss one and the contravention follows the company into audits, due diligence and repatriation requests. {brand} runs this reporting calendar end to end for foreign-owned companies, start-ups with offshore investors and groups investing abroad.

## FDI Reporting: FC-GPR, FC-TRS and Form DI

The core inbound filings form a chain from money to cap table:

| Filing | Trigger | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Advance reporting form (ARF) | Receipt of inward remittance from the foreign investor | Within 30 days of receipt |
| Form FC-GPR | Allotment of shares or eligible instruments to the non-resident | Within 30 days of allotment |
| Form FC-TRS | Transfer of shares between a resident and a non-resident | Within 60 days of the transfer or remittance of consideration |
| Form DI | Downstream investment by a foreign-owned or controlled {country} entity into another {country} entity | Within 30 days of the investment |

All filings run through the Single Master Form on the RBI FIRMS portal, which requires a one-time Entity Master Form registration. Pricing-guideline checks and valuation certifications attach to transfer filings, and our [FDI in companies and LLPs](/fdi-in-company-llp) guide explains the underlying ownership rules.

## The Annual FLA Return: July 15, Every Year

The Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets is due by July 15 each year for every entity with FDI on its books or an overseas investment outstanding. The return reconciles shareholding, reserves, external borrowings and overseas assets to the audited balance sheet — and a nil return is still mandatory. Persistent non-filing attracts penalty action and can block future remittances, which is why we reconcile FLA data before the return is submitted.

## APR and ODI Reporting for Outbound Structures

Reporting also runs in the opposite direction. Branch and liaison offices of foreign companies file an annual performance report through their AD bank each year. {country} companies investing overseas report acquisitions and disinvestments in Form FC under the Overseas Investment Rules and file an annual performance report for the foreign entity by December 31. Resident individuals remitting under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme — up to USD 250,000 per financial year — need correct purpose coding and TCS handling. Our [foreign subsidiary tax planning](/foreign-subsidiary-tax-planning) practice addresses the tax side of these outbound structures.

## FEMA Compounding and the Penalty Framework

Under Section 13 of FEMA 1999, a contravention attracts penalty up to three times the amount involved — or up to two lakh rupees where the amount is not quantifiable — with further daily penalties for continuing contraventions. Section 15 provides the remedy: compounding, a voluntary application admitting the contravention and paying the composition fee. Late FC-GPR, missed FLA returns and unreported share transfers are all routinely compounded when handled promptly. We prepare the application, quantify the exposure, appear through the process and rebuild the filing discipline afterwards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Form FC-GPR and when is it filed?

Form FC-GPR reports the issue or transfer of eligible instruments to a non-resident investor. It is due within 30 days of allotment and is filed on the RBI FIRMS portal through the AD Category-I bank, after the inward remittance has been reported in the advance reporting form.

### Who has to file the annual FLA return?

Every {country} company, LLP or other entity that has received foreign direct investment or made overseas investment must file the Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets by July 15 each year — even when the previous year had no transactions, a nil return is mandatory.

### What happens if FEMA reporting was missed or delayed?

A delayed filing is a contravention of FEMA 1999. Regularisation requires an application to the Reserve Bank of {country} for compounding, supported by reasons and the overdue filings. Penalties can extend to three times the amount involved, so early voluntary disclosure matters.

### Can an {country} company set up a subsidiary abroad without RBI approval?

Yes, within limits. Under the Overseas Investment Rules, an {country} company can make overseas direct investment through the automatic route up to the prescribed net-worth-linked limits, and must report it in Form FC through its AD bank. Larger or strategic commitments need Reserve Bank approval.

### What is the Liberalised Remittance Scheme?

LRS allows resident individuals to remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year for permitted current and capital account transactions, including overseas investments. Remittances above the limit require Reserve Bank of {country} approval, and LRS remittances attract tax-collected-at-source rules.
