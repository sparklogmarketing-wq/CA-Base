> Compliance forms and checklists are the prescribed statutory formats — GST returns, income tax returns, TDS statements and ROC filings — through which Indian businesses report activity to regulators, together with the pre-submission checks that ensure each form is complete, accurate and filed within the prescribed time.

## How to Use This List of Compliance Forms and Checklists

This reference lists the forms an Indian business meets most often, grouped by regulator. Forms are issued and revised by the {direct_tax_authority}, {indirect_tax_authority} and {corporate_affairs_authority}, so confirm the current version on the official portal when filing. Pair each form with the matching guide on this site for thresholds, due dates and process.

## GST Forms at a Glance

A regular taxpayer's exposure is mostly GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B; the other forms apply by taxpayer category or transaction type. The full cycle, including QRMP options, is in our [GST return filing](/gst-return-filing) guide.

| Form | Who files it | What it reports |
|---|---|---|
| GSTR-1 | Regular taxpayers | Outward supplies detail, monthly or quarterly |
| GSTR-3B | Regular taxpayers | Summary return with tax payment |
| CMP-08 | Composition taxpayers | Quarterly statement-cum-challan with payment |
| GSTR-4 | Composition taxpayers | Annual return |
| GSTR-9 | Regular taxpayers | Annual return consolidating the year |
| GSTR-9C | Large taxpayers | Self-certified reconciliation filed with GSTR-9 |
| GSTR-7 | GST tax deductors | TDS deducted under GST |
| GSTR-8 | E-commerce operators | TCS collected on supplies through the platform |
| GST REG-01 | Applicants | Application for GST registration |

## Income Tax and TDS Forms

Return selection follows the taxpayer's income heads, not its size. Reconcile Form 26AS, AIS and TIS against books before any filing — our [AIS & Form 26AS guide](/ais-sft-guide) shows how.

| Form | Used for |
|---|---|
| ITR-1 | Resident individuals — salary, one house property, other income up to Rs 50 lakh |
| ITR-2 | Individuals/HUFs with capital gains, multiple house properties or foreign income |
| ITR-3 | Individuals/HUFs with business or professional income |
| ITR-4 | Presumptive taxation under sections 44AD, 44ADA and 44AE |
| ITR-5 | Firms, LLPs, AOPs and BOIs |
| ITR-6 | Companies other than those exempt under section 11 |
| ITR-7 | Trusts and institutions exempt under sections 11 to 13 |
| Form 26AS / AIS / TIS | Annual tax credit and information statements |
| Form 3CB–3CD | Tax audit report under section 44AB |
| 24Q / 26Q / 27Q | Quarterly TDS returns: salary, resident non-salary, non-resident payments |
| 27EQ | Quarterly TCS return |
| Form 16 / 16A | Annual salary and periodical TDS certificates |
| 15CA / 15CB | Foreign remittance reporting with the CA certificate |

## ROC and Companies Act Forms

Private companies carry a fixed annual set under the Companies Act 2013, filed on the {corporate_affairs_authority} portal — see [ROC compliance](/roc-compliance) for the calendar and [tax audit](/tax-audit) where section 44AB also applies:

- **AOC-4** — audited financial statements, filed within 30 days of the AGM
- **MGT-7 / MGT-7A** — annual return (MGT-7A for OPCs and small companies)
- **DIR-3 KYC** — annual KYC for every director holding a DIN
- **DPT-3** — return of money received that does not form part of share capital
- **MSME Form 1** — half-yearly reporting of delayed payments to micro and small suppliers
- **ADT-1** — appointment of the statutory auditor
- **INC-20A** — declaration before commencement of business
- **PAS-3** — return of allotment
- **CHG-1** — registration of charges created on company assets
- **CHG-4 / CHG-8 / CHG-9** — satisfaction of charges, condonation of delay and charges on debentures
- **SH-7** — alteration of share capital
- **INC-27** — conversion between company classes
- **MR-1** — return of managing director, whole-time director or manager appointment
- **DIR-6 / DIR-11 / DIR-12** — director particulars, resignation and appointment or cessation
- **MSC-1 / MSC-3 / MSC-4** — dormant status application, annual return and reactivation
- **INC-22A** — one-time ACTIVE tagging of the registered office and directors
- **GNL-2 / GNL-4** — general document submissions and addenda to filed forms
- **LLP Forms 5, 15 and 24** — LLP name change, registered office change and striking off
- **DPT-4** — statement of deposits existing on the commencement of the Act
- **NDH-1 / NDH-2 / NDH-3 / NDH-4** — Nidhi statutory compliance return, extension of time, half-yearly return and Nidhi declaration
- **FC-2 / FC-3 / FC-4** — foreign company alteration, annual accounts and annual return

Employers run a parallel monthly cycle: PF through the ECR, ESI contributions, state-specific professional tax, and salary TDS deposited by the seventh of the following month, followed by quarterly 24Q returns and annual Form 16, plus registers under the gratuity, bonus and wage laws. ## Pre-Filing Checklists That Prevent Notices

- **GST:** reconcile purchases against GSTR-2B before claiming ITC, confirm the section 16(2) conditions, and verify e-invoice and HSN data on outbound invoices.
- **Income tax:** match Form 26AS, AIS and TIS to books, and provide for advance tax at 15, 45, 75 and 100 per cent by June 15, September 15, December 15 and March 15.
- **Audit-ready:** keep signed financial statements, board minutes and valid digital signatures ready before ROC and tax audit deadlines.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which GST returns must a composition dealer file?
Composition taxpayers file CMP-08 quarterly (a statement-cum-challan with tax payment) and GSTR-4 annually. They cannot issue tax invoices or claim input credit, so the cycle is lighter than regular taxpayers'.

### Which ITR form does a company file?
Companies file ITR-6, except those claiming exemption as charitable or religious institutions, which file ITR-7. The form follows the income composition and exemption claims, not company size.

### What happens if a form is filed after the due date?
Late fees and interest apply — for example, section 234F fees on late ITRs and daily late fees on delayed GSTR-3B. Some rights, such as carried-forward losses, can also be lost.

### Do dormant or inactive companies still file ROC forms?
Yes. AOC-4, MGT-7 and DIR-3 KYC obligations continue even with no business activity; a company wanting to exit cleanly should evaluate the strike-off route instead.

### Where can I download the official forms?
Use the official portals maintained by the income tax department, the GST network and {corporate_affairs_authority}. Versions change, so download at the time of filing rather than storing old copies.
