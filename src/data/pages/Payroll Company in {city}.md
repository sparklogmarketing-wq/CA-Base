## Payroll and Statutory Compliance in {city}

Statutory payroll obligations in {state} stack up quickly, and each carries its own due date and form:

| Statute | Obligation | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EPF Act, 1952 | 12% employee and 12% employer contribution; ECR upload for establishments with 20 or more employees | Monthly |
| ESI Act, 1948 | Contribution at the prescribed rates for employees within the wage ceiling | Monthly |
| Karnataka Professional Tax | Slab-based deduction up to Rs 200 per month above the prescribed salary threshold, capped annually | Monthly, with annual return |
| TDS on salaries, Section 192 | Deposit by the 7th of the following month; quarterly Form 24Q | Monthly and quarterly |
| Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 | Provision of 15 days' wages per completed year after 5 years of service, capped at Rs 20 lakh | On separation |

Miss one and the penalties compound — interest on late PF and TDS deposits, employee complaints when Form 16 is wrong, and notices that surface years later. This is why payroll and statutory compliance belong with a CA-led team rather than a standalone data-entry vendor.
