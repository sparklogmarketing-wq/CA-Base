> Subsidiary company registration in {country} is the incorporation of a private limited company under the Companies Act 2013 in which a foreign parent holds more than 50% — often 100% — of the shares. It creates a separate {country}-incorporated legal entity that can hire staff, trade, own property and remit profits, subject to FEMA and RBI reporting.

A wholly owned subsidiary in {country} is the most common and most flexible entry vehicle for foreign groups. It earns revenue freely, contracts in its own name, qualifies for domestic-company tax treatment and presents a clean structure for exit or listing. {brand} handles the entire cycle — incorporation, FEMA filings and the ongoing compliance stack.

## Minimum Requirements for a Subsidiary in {country}

- **Shareholders:** minimum two; the foreign parent can hold 100% in automatic-route sectors.
- **Directors:** minimum two, including at least one resident director who has stayed in {country} for 182 days or more in the previous financial year, as mandated by Section 149(3) of the Companies Act 2013.
- **Registered office:** a physical address in {country}, secured within 30 days of incorporation, with a utility bill not older than two months and the owner's no-objection certificate.
- **Parent documentation:** certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents and board resolution approving the investment, notarised or apostilled and attested at the {country} consulate where applicable.
- **Digital signatures:** DSCs for the proposed directors to sign filings on the MCA portal.

## The SPICe+ Incorporation Process

1. **Name reservation** — SPICe+ Part A reserves the company name; choose names consistent with the parent brand.
2. **SPICe+ Part B** — the integrated incorporation form covering certificate of incorporation, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC and professional tax enrolment, with e-MoA (INC-33) and e-AoA (INC-34) signed digitally.
3. **AGILE-PRO-S** — linked application for GST registration, bank account opening and, where applicable, shop-and-establishment registration.
4. **Allotment of shares to the parent** — share capital is allotted to the foreign entity against inward remittance through the AD bank.
5. **FDI reporting** — the receipt is reported to RBI, and Form FC-GPR is filed within 30 days of allotment. Our [FEMA and RBI reporting](/fema-RBI-reporting) team manages these filings on the FIRMS portal.

Once name approval and documents are in place, incorporation itself typically completes within two to four weeks, subject to registry processing.

## The Post-Incorporation Compliance Stack

The first year sets the compliance tone. Within 30 days, appoint the statutory auditor under Section 139 and convene the first board meeting. The recurring stack includes annual financial statements and returns (AOC-4, MGT-7), DIR-3 KYC for every director, DPT-3, income tax return and advance tax, GST returns where registered, and — for foreign-owned entities — the annual FLA return by July 15 and transfer pricing documentation for related-party transactions. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does subsidiary registration take in {country}?

Once parent documents are notarised or apostilled and the name is cleared, a wholly-owned subsidiary typically incorporates within two to four weeks. The certificate of incorporation, PAN and TAN issue together; allow further time for bank accounts and GST registration.

### Does an {country} subsidiary need a resident director?

Yes. Section 149(3) of the Companies Act 2013 requires at least one director who has stayed in {country} for at least 182 days in the previous financial year. A trusted employee or professional nominee can fill this role.

### What is FC-GPR and when is it due?

Form FC-GPR reports the allotment of shares to the foreign investor and is due within 30 days of allotment. It is filed on the RBI FIRMS portal through the AD Category-I bank; delays can require FEMA compounding.

### Can the foreign parent own 100% of the subsidiary?

In most sectors, yes — FDI up to 100% is allowed under the automatic route. A few sectors carry caps, approval requirements or conditions, and investments from countries sharing a land border with {country} need Government approval under Press Note 3.

### Is there a minimum capital requirement for a subsidiary?

No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed under the Companies Act 2013. In practice the parent funds working capital based on the business plan, and FDI pricing guidelines apply when capital is brought in as equity.

### What compliance follows incorporation?

Appoint the first auditor within 30 days, hold board meetings, file AOC-4 and MGT-7 annually, complete DIR-3 KYC for directors, file the income tax return and GST returns where registered, FC-GPR after FDI and the annual FLA return by July 15.
