> Branch office registration in {country} is the approval process under FEMA 1999 by which a foreign company establishes a place of business in {country} through an AD Category-I bank. A branch office may earn income from permitted activities, while a liaison office acts purely as a representative channel and cannot undertake any commercial activity.

Where a full subsidiary is premature, a branch or liaison office gives the parent a regulated foothold: a branch office to execute specific business activities, a liaison office to study the market and represent the brand. {brand} manages the approval, registration and annual reporting for both, and advises when the [subsidiary route](/subsidiary-entity) is the better vehicle.

## Branch Office Registration in {country}: Eligibility and Approval

The parent must satisfy a track-record test: profit-making during the immediately preceding five financial years and net worth of USD 100,000 or more. The application — supported by the parent's audited accounts, certificate of incorporation, board resolution and the proposed activities — is submitted in the prescribed form through an AD Category-I bank to the Reserve Bank of {country}. Processing time is not statutorily fixed; well-prepared applications typically clear within a few weeks.

Branch offices may undertake activities such as export and import of goods, professional and consultancy services, research, promoting technical or financial collaborations, representing the parent, IT and software development, and technical support for the parent's products. Retail trading of any kind is expressly outside a branch office's permitted scope.

## Liaison Office: The Representational Route

A liaison office suits groups that want a market presence without revenue: parent profitability in the last three financial years and net worth of USD 50,000 or more. The office can represent the parent, promote export and import to and from {country}, facilitate technical and financial collaborations and act as a communication channel — nothing more. All expenses are met from inward remittances from the parent, and approval is normally granted for an initial period of three years, renewable.

## Permitted Activities: Branch vs Liaison vs Project Office

| Parameter | Branch office | Liaison office | Project office |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Specific business activities | Representation and market study | Executing a specific contract |
| Revenue in {country} | Permitted, limited to approved activities | None | Only from the project |
| Parent eligibility | 5 profit years; net worth USD 100,000+ | 3 profit years; net worth USD 50,000+ | Relaxed where the project contract meets specified conditions |
| Funding | Own income and parent remittances | Parent remittances only | Project remittances from abroad |
| Taxability | Foreign-company rates on {country}-source profits | Generally no taxable income | Taxed as business income for the project |

Project offices suit foreign companies executing specific contracts in {country}; where the project is funded by direct inward remittance or by a bilateral or multilateral agreement, the parent-track-record conditions are relaxed.

## Form FC-1 and Post-Approval Registrations

Once the office is established, the branch or liaison office must be registered with the Registrar of Companies in Form FC-1 within 30 days, followed by PAN and TAN, a local office address and a designated authorised representative. The {country} bank account is funded exclusively by remittances from the parent. ## Validity, Renewals and Annual Reporting

Compliance does not end at approval. Every branch and liaison office files an annual performance report through its AD bank, together with audited financial statements, and liaison office approvals are renewed on expiry of their initial tenure. Continuous reporting to the Reserve Bank of {country} sits inside our wider [FEMA and RBI reporting](/fema-RBI-reporting) calendar.

## Taxability of Branch and Liaison Offices

A branch office is part of the foreign company for tax purposes: {country}-source profits are taxed at the rates applicable to foreign companies, and after-tax branch profits can be repatriated to the parent. A liaison office that confines itself to representational activity generates no taxable income, but it still must file a return of income each year. Where a branch carries on ongoing business, permanent establishment analysis under the parent's DTAA becomes critical — our [foreign company entry guide](/foreign-company-entry-india) covers this in the wider context.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between a branch office and a liaison office?

A branch office can undertake limited revenue-earning activities such as export-import, consultancy and IT services. A liaison office only represents the parent, promotes its interests and channels communication — it cannot earn any income in {country} and is funded entirely by parent remittances.

### What are the eligibility requirements for a branch office?

The foreign parent must have been profit-making for the last five financial years and hold net worth of USD 100,000 or more. The application goes to the Reserve Bank of {country} through an AD Category-I bank in the prescribed form.

### Can a liaison office earn revenue in {country}?

No. A liaison office cannot undertake commercial, trading or industrial activity and cannot earn income in {country}. Its operating expenses must be met through inward remittances from the parent, and it must file an annual performance report with its AD bank.

### What is Form FC-1 and when is it due?

Form FC-1 registers the established branch or liaison office with the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2013. It must be filed within 30 days of establishing the office, along with the parent company details and authorised representative particulars.

### Do branch offices pay tax in {country}?

Yes. Branch office profits attributable to {country} operations are taxed at the rates applicable to foreign companies, which are higher than domestic rates. A liaison office confined to representational activities generally has no taxable income but must still file its income tax return.

### Can a branch office do retail trading?

No. Branch offices of foreign companies cannot undertake retail trading activities of any kind in {country}. Retail entry requires a subsidiary or joint venture structure, subject to the sectoral conditions for FDI in multi-brand retail.
