> Accounting outsourcing services transfer a company's day-to-day bookkeeping, month-end close, reconciliations and management reporting to a specialist firm. A CA-supervised team runs accounts payable and receivable, maintains the general ledger on platforms such as Tally or Zoho Books, and delivers MIS packs on a fixed monthly calendar.

## What Accounting Outsourcing Services Include

Outsourcing moves the transactional finance function — the ledger, the payables and receivables cycles, payroll accounting inputs and reconciliation — to a team that runs it to a calendar under chartered accountant review. A typical scope:

- Sales, purchase, journal and cash or bank vouchers recorded in the accounting system
- Vendor bill processing, scheduled payment runs and accounts payable ageing
- Customer invoicing, receipt application and accounts receivable follow-up
- Bank, cash, party and inter-ledger reconciliations
- Fixed asset register and depreciation schedules
- Payroll accounting entries, with processing itself handled through our [payroll services](/payroll-services)
- GST-ready sales and purchase registers that feed the indirect tax filings

## The Monthly Close Cycle

A published close calendar is what separates numbers you can steer by from numbers that arrive after the decisions are made.| Timeline | Activity |
|---|---|
| Days 1-3 | Cut-off: vendor bills, sales invoices, expense claims and vouchers for the month captured in the ledger |
| Days 3-5 | Bank, cash, party and inter-ledger reconciliations; GST-ready registers locked |
| Days 5-7 | Accruals, prepayments, depreciation and provisions posted; payroll entries recorded |
| Days 7-9 | Trial balance review by the supervising CA; fixed asset and inventory checks |
| Days 9-10 | MIS pack issued — profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, ageing and variance notes — with a review call |

That rhythm is what accounting outsourcing services are built to deliver.

## Accounts Payable, Receivable and Reconciliation

On the **payables** side, bills are coded to the right cost centre, vendor ledgers are aged weekly, and payment runs follow an approval workflow rather than ad-hoc requests. On the **receivables** side, invoices go out on time, receipts are applied against the correct invoices, and stale debtors are escalated with an ageing and collection note.

Reconciliation discipline is the backbone: every bank account every month, party ledgers confirmed at set intervals, and differences escalated with a reason code instead of being rolled forward. Clean reconciliations are also what keep GST input tax credit claims and income tax positions defensible — see our [income tax services](/income-tax) for the filing side.

## The Cloud Accounting Stack: Tally, Zoho Books and QuickBooks

We work on the platform the business already uses — TallyPrime, Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online or comparable systems. Support covers chart-of-accounts design, migration from legacy data, integration with banking and billing tools, role-based access, and a maintained audit trail so every edit is traceable. Cloud working matters for distributed teams: documents flow in through shared capture tools, and founders see live dashboards rather than waiting for month-end files.

## Management Reporting and MIS Deliverables

The standard monthly pack contains a profit and loss statement with budget variance, a balance sheet, a cash flow statement, debtor and creditor ageing, and a one-page commentary explaining material movements. Where deeper analysis helps, we add unit- or project-level profitability, working capital dashboards and board-format reporting — the [finance controller and MIS](/finance-controller-mis) mandate builds directly on this base.

## Pricing Models for Outsourced Accounting

Providers typically price one of three ways:

- **Monthly retainer** for a defined transaction profile and deliverables list
- **Volume-linked pricing** tracking invoices, vouchers or bank lines processed
- **Entity or hybrid pricing** for groups with multiple companies or branches

Quotes should always be tied to a written scope — close calendar, MIS format, compliance touchpoints — so scope creep and fee disputes are prevented at the outset. {brand} quotes after reviewing transaction volumes, entity structure and the standard your books must meet, including the records a [tax audit](/tax-audit) under section 44AB will rely on.

## Why Outsource to a CA-Supervised Team

Accounting outsourcing services work best when preparation and review are deliberately separated:

- **Segregation of duties** — preparation by accountants, independent review by a CA
- **Continuity** — no single-accountant dependency, leave gaps or attrition risk
- **Compliance-ready books** — records that withstand GST notices, tax audits and due diligence
- **Backlog clean-up** — prior-period accounting reconstructed and brought current before the new year begins
