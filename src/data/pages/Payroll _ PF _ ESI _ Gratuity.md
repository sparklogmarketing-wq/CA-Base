## What Payroll Outsourcing Services Include

Payroll is both a computation exercise and a compliance obligation. An outsourced mandate covers both sides:

- **Inputs** — attendance and leave data, new joins and exits, salary structure changes, reimbursements and incentive declarations
- **Computation** — gross-to-net salary working with every statutory deduction applied correctly
- **Outputs** — payslips, a bank payment file, payroll registers and accounting entries into the books
- **Compliance** — PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS challans and returns, filed on the statutory calendar
- **Year-end** — gratuity provisioning review, Form 16 generation and payroll reconciliation for the [tax audit](/tax-audit) file

## The Monthly Payroll Cycle, Step by Step

1. **Input collection** — attendance, leave balances and master-data changes locked on a fixed cut-off date
2. **Gross-to-net computation** — basic pay, allowances, overtime and incentives net of PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS and voluntary deductions
3. **Review and sign-off** — variance against the previous month checked and approved before release
4. **Payment and payslips** — bank transfer file generated and payslips distributed to employees
5. **Statutory remittance** — PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS challans paid within due dates
6. **Accounting and MIS** — payroll journal entries posted and headcount cost reported to management

## Statutory Contributions: PF, ESI, Gratuity, PT and TDS

| Statute | Employer pays | Employee contributes | Key thresholds |
|---|---|---|---|
| EPF Act 1952 | 12% of PF wages | 12% of PF wages | Mandatory up to the wage ceiling of Rs 15,000 a month; of the employer share, 8.33% (capped) routes to the pension scheme and the balance to the provident fund |
| ESI Act 1948 | 3.25% of wages | 0.75% of wages | Applies in implemented areas to establishments with 10 or more employees; covers employees earning up to Rs 21,000 a month (Rs 25,000 for employees with disability) |
| Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 | Funded or provisioned by the employer | — | Payable after 5 years of continuous service; 15/26 of last drawn wages per completed year, capped at Rs 20 lakh |
| Profession tax ({state}) | Per {state} slabs where levied | Deducted per {state} slabs | State-specific slabs, registration and periodic remittance rules |
| Income-tax Act 1961, section 192 | Deposits TDS monthly | Tax at slab rates | Estimated annual salary less declarations; quarterly Form 24Q; Form 16 by 15 June |

Three points employers frequently get wrong. First, PF: employees drawing above the Rs 15,000 ceiling may, per EPFO rules, be excluded from the statutory contribution or opt to continue — the treatment must be consistent with the employment terms. Second, gratuity liability accrues with every completed year even though payment happens at exit, so monthly provisioning avoids year-end surprises. Third, TDS under section 192 is not a flat percentage: it follows each employee's projected annual tax after declarations for house rent, investments and the chosen regime.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When is PF due each month?

PF contributions along with the electronic challan-cum-return are due by the 15th of the following month. Late payment attracts interest and damages under the EPF Act, so remittance is tracked on a fixed compliance calendar.

### Who must register under ESI?

Establishments in implemented areas employing 10 or more people must register. Employees earning up to Rs 21,000 a month (Rs 25,000 for employees with disability) are covered, with contributions of 3.25 percent employer and 0.75 percent employee.

### When does gratuity become payable?

After 5 years of continuous service, on resignation, retirement or termination. It is computed at 15/26 of last drawn wages per completed year, capped at Rs 20 lakh, and paid within 30 days of becoming due.

### What is TDS under section 192?

Employers deduct tax on salary at slab rates on estimated annual income after declarations and prescribed deductions, deposit it monthly, file quarterly Form 24Q returns and issue Form 16 by 15 June each year.

### Can you handle payroll for employees in multiple states?

Yes. Each state's professional tax rules are mapped and remitted separately, while PF, ESI and TDS follow the central framework — one payroll register, one state-wise compliance calendar.
