> Taxation of real estate and construction companies in India runs through works-contract GST, a TDS matrix covering contractors, property buyers and tenants, restricted input credit, RERA escrow discipline and distinct joint-development treatment for landowners versus developers. Rate positions and sunset clauses move often, so invoicing and approval dates decide outcomes.

## Why Is Taxation of Real Estate and Construction Companies in India So Fragmented?

A single project sits inside GST, the Income-tax Act, RERA, stamp-duty law and labour statutes at once, and each regime answers what is being sold differently. GST looks at works contracts and composite supplies; income tax looks at percentage-of-completion revenue and deductions tied to approval dates; RERA looks at escrow and disclosure. Managing the seams — not any one statute — is where real estate and construction tax outcomes are decided.

## How Does GST Apply to Works Contracts and Construction Services?

A works contract under Section 2(119) of the CGST Act is a composite supply of construction services with transfer of goods. Classification follows the property served — residential, commercial or infrastructure — each with different rate and credit outcomes, and the 2025 rationalisation into the 5% and 18% slabs makes rate verification at invoicing essential.

| Transaction | GST position |
|---|---|
| Under-construction residential unit | Taxable composite supply; concessional route without input credit; exempt once sold with occupancy or completion certificate |
| Commercial construction for a client | Works contract service, taxable with input credit available to the recipient |
| Sale of plots | Not taxable as construction service where no construction service is involved |
| Works contract to government bodies | Specific valuation and TDS interfaces under Section 51 |

Reverse-charge exposure also sits quietly in project finances: development rights, TDR and floor-space transactions attract tax under Section 9(3) mechanics, and labour contractors can pull the builder into reverse charge. Developer-side detail sits in our [GST in real estate guide](/gst-real-estate); sector frictions are catalogued under [GST sector issues](/gst-sector-issues).

## Which TDS Provisions Apply Across a Construction Project?

| Section | Who deducts | On what | Key mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|
| 194C | Builders, contractors, businesses | Payments to contractors and sub-contractors | Deduct at prescribed rates (lower for individual or HUF payees); check Section 206AB higher rate for non-filers; report in 26Q |
| 194-IA | Buyer of immovable property | Consideration of Rs 50 lakh or more | 1% at credit or payment; deposit via Form 26QB |
| 194-IB | Tenant (individual or HUF) | Monthly rent above Rs 50,000 | Deduct at the prescribed rate once a year from prior months; deposit via Form 26QC |
| 51 (GST TDS) | Government and notified undertakings | Works contract and HSN-tagged payments | Deduct at the prescribed rate; report in GSTR-7 |

Compliance fails most often on sub-contractor chains: the principal contractor must gross up its own liability over downstream deductions, and payee PAN errors trigger the higher Section 206AA rate; non-filer counterparties bring Section 206AB into play.

## What Input Credit Can Developers Actually Use?

Credit strategy must match the project's billing route. Residential projects on the concessional without-credit route forfeit input credit entirely, making procurement a direct margin item. Commercial and mixed-use projects claim credit on inputs and input services attributable to taxable supplies — never on land — while credit allocable to exempt supplies reverses under Rule 42-style computations. Tag contractor invoices, consultancy and equipment hire to the project and supply type at entry, not at year-end; the mechanics sit in our [GST ITC and RCM guide](/gst-itc-rcm).

## What About Affordable Housing Deductions and RERA?

Affordable housing incentive planning is now mostly retrospective. Section 80-IBA allowed a profits-linked deduction for affordable residential projects, but the approval windows have lapsed — current claims generally concern legacy projects approved within those dates that still satisfy completion conditions, so preserve approval letters and completion evidence.

RERA is entirely present-tense: project registration before advertising, 70% of buyer receipts in a designated escrow account linked to land and construction cost, quarterly disclosures and annual audit of project accounts. Escrow fails audits when withdrawals exceed certified expenditure, so finance and engineering sign-offs must move together. Detail sits in our [RERA and JDA taxation guide](/rera-jda-real-estate).

## How Are Joint Development and Revenue-Sharing Deals Treated?

Where a landowner contributes land and a developer builds, both sides book different transactions out of the same project: the developer may owe GST on development rights received, while the landowner faces capital-gains consequences at completion, with separate treatment for monetary components. Revenue-sharing and area-sharing variants change the allocation again. The side-by-side comparison — including Section 194-IC deduction — sits in the dedicated [RERA and JDA guide](/rera-jda-real-estate), and year-end positions in our [income tax services](/income-tax).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GST applicable on sale of under-construction property?

Yes. Sale of an under-construction unit is a composite works contract attracting GST, with land treated as part of the composite supply. Units sold after completion along with the occupancy or completion certificate are exempt.

### Who deducts TDS under Section 194-IA?

The buyer of immovable property deducts 1% where total consideration is Rs 50 lakh or more, at credit or payment, whichever is earlier, and deposits it through Form 26QB. Rural agricultural land is outside the provision.

### Can builders claim input tax credit?

It depends on the supply. Residential projects billed on the without-ITC route get concessional treatment but no credit, while commercial construction allows input credit on inputs and input services used for taxable supplies. Land itself never carries credit.

### What is a works contract under GST?

Section 2(119) of the CGST Act defines it as a composite supply of contracts for building, construction, fabrication, completion, erection, installation, fitting out or improvement of immovable property, where the contract also involves transfer of goods.

### Is the affordable housing deduction still available to developers?

Section 80-IBA allowed a profits deduction for approved affordable housing projects, but the approval windows have lapsed. Current claims generally concern legacy projects already approved within the notified dates, subject to their completion conditions.

### What RERA obligations affect a developer's finances?

Project registration, maintaining 70% of buyer receipts in a designated escrow account linked to project land and construction cost, quarterly progress disclosures and annual audit of project accounts. Escrow withdrawals must match certified project expenditure.
