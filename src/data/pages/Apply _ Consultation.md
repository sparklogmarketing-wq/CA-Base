Applying online is the fastest way to put a qualified chartered accountant on your matter. Describe what you need — an audit quotation, help with a GST notice, incorporation of a new company or ongoing monthly compliance — and our team schedules a CA consultation in Bangalore with the relevant specialist, at our office or by video call. ## What You Can Consult Us On

- Statutory audit, tax audit under section 44AB and internal audit engagements
- Income tax return filing, tax planning, TDS compliance and notice or assessment representation
- GST registration, monthly and annual returns, input tax credit reconciliation and notices
- Company, LLP and OPC incorporation — see our [company registration services](/company-registration)
- Payroll processing, ROC compliance and outsourced bookkeeping
- Virtual CFO support, MIS and fundraising-ready financial reporting

## The Engagement Process

1. **Application** — you submit the enquiry with a brief on your entity and requirement.
2. **Discovery call** — a specialist clarifies scope, volumes, deadlines and constraints.
3. **Scope note and fee proposal** — deliverables, timelines and commercials in writing.
4. **Engagement letter** — signed by both sides before work begins.
5. **Onboarding** — we share a document checklist from our [forms and checklists](/forms-checklists) library and diarise your statutory calendar.

## Preparing for the Call

Keep your PAN, incorporation certificate or partnership deed, latest financial statements, and any notices or prior returns handy. For GST matters include recent GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filings; for payroll, headcount and salary structure. Partial information still lets us arrive with useful questions.

## Fees, Payments and Timelines

Initial eligibility discussions are brief and without obligation; detailed advisory is scheduled formally with the fee confirmed first. Invoices follow the terms of your engagement letter, and accepted payment modes are on our [payment options](/payments) page. Statutory work is tracked against due dates — advance tax instalments, GSTR-3B, ITR filings and ROC forms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How soon will I hear back after applying?

Applications are routed to the relevant service lead, who responds to schedule your discovery call. Peak compliance seasons may extend timelines slightly, but every enquiry is acknowledged.

### What happens after the discovery call?

We issue a written scope note and fee proposal. Once you accept, an engagement letter is signed and onboarding begins with a document checklist and an agreed filing calendar.

### Can I consult about more than one service?

Yes. Applications often cover combined needs such as company registration with GST, or audit alongside income tax returns. We coordinate internally so you deal with one engagement team.

### Is the consultation online or in person?

Either. Most first consultations happen by video or phone; office meetings in Bangalore are available by appointment when documents or face-to-face discussion make it worthwhile.

### Is there a fee for the first consultation?

Initial eligibility discussions are generally brief and without obligation. Detailed advisory sessions are scheduled formally, with any fee confirmed and agreed before the session.
