## When Is Internal Audit Mandatory in India?

Section 138 read with the Companies (Accounts) Rules prescribes internal audit for the following classes of company. Even where the mandate does not apply, lenders, investors and group audit teams increasingly require an independent internal audit function as a governance marker.

| Company class | Threshold requiring internal audit |
|---|---|
| Listed companies | All listed companies |
| Unlisted public companies | Paid-up capital of Rs 50 crore or more, or turnover of Rs 200 crore or more, or outstanding loans or borrowings of Rs 100 crore or more, or outstanding deposits of Rs 25 crore or more |
| Private companies | Paid-up capital of Rs 50 crore or more, or turnover of Rs 200 crore or more, or outstanding loans or borrowings of Rs 100 crore or more |

## Internal Audit versus Statutory Audit

A statutory audit opines on financial statements for external stakeholders; internal audit examines whether internal controls prevent and detect error and fraud, for management and the board. The first is an annual opinion under the Companies Act 2013; the second is a continuous discipline, mandatory for threshold companies under section 138. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is internal audit mandatory for my company?

For listed companies, yes. Unlisted public companies need it above thresholds of Rs 50 crore paid-up capital, Rs 200 crore turnover, Rs 100 crore borrowings or Rs 25 crore deposits. Private companies need it above Rs 50 crore capital, Rs 200 crore turnover or Rs 100 crore borrowings.

### How often should internal audit be performed?

Frequency is risk-based. Larger companies typically run quarterly cycles covering high-risk areas, while mid-size companies opt for half-yearly or annual reviews. Lenders and investors may also specify cadence in sanction letters or shareholder agreements.

### Can we outsource internal audit to a CA firm?

Yes. Section 138 allows the audit to be conducted by a chartered accountant in practice. Outsourcing gives you independent testing, benchmarked methodologies and senior review without recruiting a full internal audit team.

### What is a revenue leakage audit?

A focused internal audit that traces revenue from order to cash — pricing, discounts, credit notes, unbilled services and reconciliations — to identify and quantify losses from process gaps, errors or manipulation, and recommend corrective controls.
